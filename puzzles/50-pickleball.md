## Introduction

The rec center runs a pickleball ladder on Tuesdays. Courts go by start time, and the sign-up sheet gets rewritten more than once.
Five players are involved. Determine each player's start time, paddle, serve and gripe.
- **Player**: Yolanda, Pete Ng, Ramesh, Arlene and Buzz Kovacs.
- **Start time** (listed from earlier to later): 8am, 9am, 10am, 11am and noon.
- **Paddle**: an oversize paddle, a bright orange paddle, a carbon-fiber paddle, a graphite paddle and a wooden loaner.
- **Serve**: a high lob, a lefty slice, the same serve every time, a flat drive and a spin serve.
- **Gripe**: a bad knee, a late night, the noisy court next door, nobody at all and a dead ball.
Every player has a different start time, paddle, serve and gripe, so within each category every item is used exactly once.
