## Introduction

There are 5 entries. Each has exactly one placing and one item from every other category below, and within each category every item belongs to a different entry.
- **Placing** (listed in order): first, second, third, fourth and fifth.
- **Builder**: Hartley, Fenwick, Juniper, Khalid and Aleksy.
- **Build**: a hollow lighthouse, a sleeping dragon, a cathedral with flying buttresses, a moated keep and a recumbent walrus.
- **Chief tool**: a cake slice, a paint scraper, a soup spoon, a child's rake and a pastry brush.
- **Undone by**: structural overreach, a loose dog, a gull, a rival's elbow and a cricket ball.
The places above are listed in order, from first to fifth.

## Clues

1. Fourth goes with a paint scraper.
2. Second and a loose dog are exactly three places apart in placing order. A sleeping dragon's placing is immediately better than second, with no placing in between.
3. Fifth and a cake slice are exactly four places apart in placing order. Fifth and a child's rake are exactly two places apart in placing order. Khalid falls somewhere between a gull and a cake slice in placing order.
4. One of first and a rival's elbow sits at an odd-numbered placing and the other at an even-numbered placing.
5. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) A recumbent walrus and Khalid are next to each other in placing order, in one order or the other. (b) Fourth is immediately better than a moated keep's placing, with no placing in between.
6. A cricket ball falls somewhere between Hartley and third in placing order.
7. Juniper's placing is immediately better than second, with no placing in between.
8. The following two do NOT both hold; either one alone may hold, and so may neither: (a) A recumbent walrus does not go with fourth. (b) A sleeping dragon does not go with a pastry brush.
9. Fourth falls somewhere between a cake slice and a pastry brush in placing order.
10. A moated keep and Aleksy are next to each other in placing order, in one order or the other.
11. A hollow lighthouse does not go with Fenwick.
