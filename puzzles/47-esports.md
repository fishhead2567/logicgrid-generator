## Introduction

The school esports club booked the library after hours. Six players, a double-elimination bracket, and a very long argument about seeding.
Six players are involved. Determine each player's exit round, game, gear and tag.
- **Player**: Gus Villalobos, Beau, Tyrell, Oksana, Saoirse and Mina.
- **Exit round** (listed from earlier to later): round one, round two, round three, round four, round five and round six.
- **Game**: the rhythm game, the soccer sim, the platformer, the kart racer, the puzzle brawler and the block-builder.
- **Gear**: an arcade stick, a wireless headset, a borrowed controller, a tournament pad, the club's old mouse and a split keyboard.
- **Tag**: Static, Marmalade, Downhill, Pocketwatch, Nine Lives and BlueHeron.
Every player has a different exit round, game, gear and tag, so within each category every item is used exactly once.
