## Introduction

There are 5 members. Each has exactly one day and one item from every other category below, and within each category every item belongs to a different member.
- **Day** (listed in order): Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday.
- **Knitter**: Serafina, Maudie, Angus, Ebba and Lionel.
- **Project**: a shawl of impossible lace, a pair of argyle socks, a cable-knit blanket, a dog coat and a fisherman's gansey.
- **Yarn**: mill-end acrylic, nettle fibre, oily Guernsey wool, recycled sari silk and undyed Shetland.
- **Setback**: a cat, unexplained shrinkage, a misread chart, running out of dye lot and a broken circular needle.
The days above are listed in order, from Monday to Friday.

1. Wednesday goes with a broken circular needle. A pair of argyle socks and a broken circular needle are exactly two days apart in day order.

3. Tuesday and Maudie are exactly two days apart in day order. Maudie and a cable-knit blanket are next to each other in day order, in one order or the other. Maudie falls somewhere between a misread chart and unexplained shrinkage in day order.
4. A fisherman's gansey, running out of dye lot and undyed Shetland are three different members. Friday and undyed Shetland are exactly three days apart in day order. Maudie's day is earlier than a fisherman's gansey's day.
5. The following two do NOT both hold; either one alone may hold, and so may neither: (a) Tuesday is earlier than a broken circular needle's day. (b) Angus goes with a misread chart.
6. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Ebba and a cable-knit blanket are next to each other in day order, in one order or the other. (b) A cable-knit blanket and a misread chart are next to each other in day order, in one order or the other.
7. Recycled sari silk's day is earlier than a cable-knit blanket's day.
8. Ebba's day is earlier than a misread chart's day. Ebba does not go with a dog coat.
9. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Serafina and mill-end acrylic are next to each other in day order, in one order or the other. (b) Serafina's day is immediately earlier than nettle fibre's day, with no day in between.
