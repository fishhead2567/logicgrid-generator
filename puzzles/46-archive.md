## Introduction

The Vance Municipal Archive was reorganised in 1974 by somebody who has since left no forwarding address.
Six archivists are involved. Determine each archivist's shelf, holding, decade and condition.
- **Archivist**: Dr Lindqvist, Ms Okafor, Mr Pelletier, Ms Castellano, Ms Bhattacharya and Dr Mwangi.
- **Shelf** (listed from lower to higher): the bottom shelf, the second shelf, the third shelf, the fourth shelf, the fifth shelf and the top shelf.
- **Holding**: a folder of libel writs, a set of tram timetables, the harbour dredging reports, the lost property register, the smallpox ledgers and the workhouse admissions book.
- **Decade**: the 1950s, the 1860s, the 1940s, the 1970s, the 1830s and the 1890s.
- **Condition**: mouse-chewed, over-repaired with tape, immaculate, water-damaged, foxed and bound upside down.
Every archivist has a different shelf, holding, decade and condition, so within each category every item is used exactly once.
