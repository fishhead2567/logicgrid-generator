# The Mislabelled Drawers

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 6 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 16 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `58cffe6ce7a7`

## Introduction

The Vance Municipal Archive was reorganised in 1974 by somebody who has since left no forwarding address.
Six archivists are involved. Determine each archivist's shelf, holding, decade and condition.
- **Archivist**: Dr Lindqvist, Ms Okafor, Mr Pelletier, Ms Castellano, Ms Bhattacharya and Dr Mwangi.
- **Shelf** (listed from lower to higher): the bottom shelf, the second shelf, the third shelf, the fourth shelf, the fifth shelf and the top shelf.
- **Holding**: a folder of libel writs, a set of tram timetables, the harbour dredging reports, the lost property register, the smallpox ledgers and the workhouse admissions book.
- **Decade**: the 1950s, the 1860s, the 1940s, the 1970s, the 1830s and the 1890s.
- **Condition**: mouse-chewed, over-repaired with tape, immaculate, water-damaged, foxed and bound upside down.
Every archivist has a different shelf, holding, decade and condition, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The archivist who covers the 1950s does not file on the bottom shelf. The archivist who describes theirs as foxed and the bottom shelf are exactly four shelves apart in shelf order.
2. The following two do NOT both hold; either one alone may hold, and so may neither: (a) The archivist who catalogues the workhouse admissions book and the third shelf are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other. (b) The archivist who covers the 1830s is higher than the bottom shelf in shelf order.
3. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Ms Castellano and the archivist who describes theirs as mouse-chewed are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other. (b) The archivist who catalogues the smallpox ledgers files on the third shelf.
4. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Mr Pelletier catalogues the workhouse admissions book. (b) The archivist who covers the 1940s files on the third shelf.
5. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) The third shelf is exactly one shelf lower than the archivist who covers the 1830s, with no shelf in between. (b) Dr Lindqvist catalogues the lost property register.
6. The archivist who describes theirs as water-damaged falls somewhere between the archivist who catalogues the lost property register and the bottom shelf in shelf order.
7. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Ms Castellano and the archivist who catalogues a set of tram timetables are exactly two shelves apart in shelf order. (b) The archivist who covers the 1950s files on the top shelf.
8. The archivist who catalogues the harbour dredging reports falls somewhere between the archivist who covers the 1970s and the second shelf in shelf order.
9. The following is NOT true: The archivist who catalogues a set of tram timetables is higher than the third shelf in shelf order.
10. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) Ms Bhattacharya does not describe theirs as immaculate. (b) The archivist who covers the 1830s is lower than the archivist who describes theirs as over-repaired with tape in shelf order.
11. The archivist who catalogues the harbour dredging reports is exactly one shelf lower than the archivist who covers the 1830s, with no shelf in between.
12. The archivist who covers the 1830s is lower than Dr Mwangi in shelf order.
13. Ms Castellano and the archivist who catalogues a folder of libel writs are exactly two shelves apart in shelf order.
14. The archivist who catalogues the workhouse admissions book is lower than the archivist who covers the 1890s in shelf order.
15. The archivist who covers the 1890s falls somewhere between Ms Okafor and the archivist who describes theirs as bound upside down in shelf order.
16. The archivist who describes theirs as water-damaged and the archivist who covers the 1890s are exactly three shelves apart in shelf order. Ms Okafor does not describe theirs as water-damaged.
