## Introduction

There are 6 drawers. Each has exactly one shelf and one item from every other category below, and within each category every item belongs to a different drawer.
- **Shelf** (listed in order): the bottom shelf, the second shelf, the third shelf, the fourth shelf, the fifth shelf and the top shelf.
- **Archivist**: Ms Okafor, Mr Pelletier, Ms Castellano, Dr Mwangi, Ms Bhattacharya and Dr Lindqvist.
- **Holding**: a folder of libel writs, a set of tram timetables, the harbour dredging reports, the lost property register, the smallpox ledgers and the workhouse admissions book.
- **Decade**: the 1950s, the 1860s, the 1940s, the 1970s, the 1830s and the 1890s.
- **Condition**: mouse-chewed, over-repaired with tape, immaculate, water-damaged, foxed and bound upside down.
The shelves above are listed in order, from the bottom shelf to the top shelf.
