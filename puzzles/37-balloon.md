# The Thistledown Balloon Ascent

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 15 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `8a8bb1bd855d`

## Introduction

The Thistledown Ascent is less a race than a slow argument conducted at two thousand feet.
Five pilots are involved. Determine each pilot's start time, envelope, landing site and ballast.
- **Pilot**: Montague, Anneliese, Horatio, Delphine and Rosalind.
- **Start time** (listed from earlier to later): 8am, 9am, 10am, 11am and noon.
- **Envelope**: the patchwork envelope, the oxblood envelope, the striped envelope, the saffron envelope and the silver envelope.
- **Landed at**: a flooded quarry, a cricket pitch, a railway siding, a cabbage field and a caravan park.
- **Ballast**: bottled water, rock salt, iron filings, lead shot and pea gravel.
Every pilot has a different start time, envelope, landing site and ballast, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The pilot who comes down in a flooded quarry is exactly one hour earlier than noon, with no start time in between. Anneliese is earlier than noon in start time order.
2. The pilot who flies the silver envelope does not launch at 11am. The pilot who flies the silver envelope does not launch at 8am.
3. 10am falls somewhere between the pilot who carries lead shot and the pilot who carries rock salt in start time order. Horatio does not launch at 10am.
4. The pilot who comes down in a cabbage field and 8am are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other.
5. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The pilot who carries pea gravel and Rosalind are exactly three hours apart in start time order. (b) Montague launches at 8am.
6. The pilot who carries pea gravel is earlier than Montague in start time order. The pilot who carries pea gravel is exactly one hour earlier than 9am, with no start time in between.
7. The pilot who carries pea gravel is earlier than the pilot who flies the oxblood envelope in start time order.
8. The pilot who flies the oxblood envelope and the pilot who comes down in a cabbage field are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other.
9. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The pilot who carries iron filings and the pilot who comes down in a caravan park are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other. (b) The pilot who flies the patchwork envelope is earlier than the pilot who carries rock salt in start time order.
10. Anneliese does not fly the oxblood envelope.
11. Delphine is exactly one hour earlier than the pilot who comes down in a railway siding, with no start time in between.
12. Anneliese does not fly the patchwork envelope.
13. The pilot who flies the striped envelope and the pilot who comes down in a flooded quarry are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other.
14. The pilot who carries bottled water and the pilot who comes down in a cabbage field are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other.
15. The pilot who carries bottled water is earlier than the pilot who comes down in a cricket pitch in start time order.
