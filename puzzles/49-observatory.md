# A Bad Week at the Pinhole Observatory

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 6 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 18 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `09f71cadaf6d`

## Introduction

The Pinhole Amateur Astronomical Society has one good telescope and a great many strong opinions about who may point it where.
Six astronomers are involved. Determine each astronomer's day, target, mishap and thermos.
- **Astronomer**: Ingrid, Beatrix, Sunniva, Cornelius, Ptolemy and Niamh.
- **Day** (listed from earlier to later): Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday.
- **Observed**: the Crab Nebula, a variable star, sunspots, a comet fragment, Saturn's rings and the Andromeda galaxy.
- **Mishap**: a dewed-up eyepiece, an unexpected cloud bank, a neighbour's floodlight, a flat battery, a dropped lens cap and a cracked filter.
- **Thermos contents**: mulled cider, oxtail soup, bone broth, black coffee, cocoa and barley water.
Every astronomer has a different day, target, mishap and thermos, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Niamh and Monday are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other. (b) Niamh and Friday both sit at odd-numbered days or both at even-numbered days.
2. Tuesday is exactly one day earlier than the astronomer who studies a variable star, with no day in between.
3. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Beatrix is exactly one day earlier than the astronomer who brings bone broth, with no day in between. (b) The astronomer who brings cocoa observes on Tuesday.
4. The astronomer who is undone by a cracked filter is exactly one day earlier than Thursday, with no day in between.
5. Ingrid is not undone by a flat battery. The astronomer who brings black coffee falls somewhere between Ingrid and Monday in day order.
6. The astronomer who is undone by a flat battery is exactly one day earlier than Friday, with no day in between.
7. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The astronomer who is undone by a neighbour's floodlight and Wednesday are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other. (b) The astronomer who studies the Crab Nebula is earlier than Wednesday in day order.
8. One of Friday and the astronomer who brings mulled cider sits at an odd-numbered day and the other at an even-numbered day.
9. The astronomer who brings black coffee and the astronomer who studies the Andromeda galaxy are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other.
10. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Beatrix and the astronomer who brings black coffee are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other. (b) Sunniva and the astronomer who is undone by a dropped lens cap are exactly four days apart in day order.
11. Cornelius is exactly one day earlier than the astronomer who is undone by a cracked filter, with no day in between.
12. The astronomer who brings oxtail soup is exactly one day earlier than the astronomer who is undone by a dewed-up eyepiece, with no day in between.
13. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The astronomer who studies sunspots is earlier than the astronomer who brings cocoa in day order. (b) The astronomer who is undone by a cracked filter and the astronomer who brings black coffee both sit at odd-numbered days or both at even-numbered days.
14. The astronomer who brings black coffee falls somewhere between Sunniva and the astronomer who is undone by an unexpected cloud bank in day order.
15. The astronomer who studies Saturn's rings is earlier than the astronomer who brings black coffee in day order.
16. Sunniva falls somewhere between the astronomer who is undone by a dropped lens cap and the astronomer who studies a comet fragment in day order.
17. Beatrix and the astronomer who brings cocoa are exactly four days apart in day order.
18. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) Cornelius and the astronomer who studies Saturn's rings are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other. (b) Ingrid and the astronomer who studies the Crab Nebula are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other.
