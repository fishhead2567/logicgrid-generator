# The Wind Is Free, the Field Is Not

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 6 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 20 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `5028cbce60ba`

## Introduction

The Dunnage Kite Festival charges by the kite, which has produced some creative definitions of 'one kite'.
Six fliers are involved. Determine each flier's entry fee, kite, mishap and line.
- **Flier**: Blaise, Morwenna, Ifeoma, Aurelio, Kipling and Gunnar.
- **Entry fee** (listed from cheaper to dearer): $2, $4, $6, $8, $10 and $12.
- **Kite**: a paper sled kite, a box kite, a hand-painted dragon, a delta wing, a seven-metre centipede and a rokkaku fighter.
- **Mishap**: a lost tail, a snapped spar, a tangled line, a sudden lull, a trespassing dog and a tree.
- **Line**: fishing monofilament, parcel string, hemp cord, waxed cotton, nylon twine and kevlar thread.
Every flier has a different entry fee, kite, mishap and line, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

2. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The flier who flies a delta wing and the flier who is undone by a sudden lull are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other. (b) Ifeoma does not pay $12.
3. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The flier who uses nylon twine is cheaper than the flier who flies a seven-metre centipede in entry fee order. (b) The flier who is undone by a sudden lull pays $4.
4. The following two do NOT both hold; either one alone may hold, and so may neither: (a) Gunnar and $8 are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other. (b) Gunnar is cheaper than the flier who flies a rokkaku fighter in entry fee order.
5. The flier who uses parcel string is dearer than $4 in entry fee order.
6. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The flier who flies a hand-painted dragon pays $6. (b) Blaise flies a hand-painted dragon.
7. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) The flier who flies a rokkaku fighter is exactly one fee step cheaper than $10, with no entry fee in between. (b) Gunnar is cheaper than the flier who flies a rokkaku fighter in entry fee order.
8. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) The flier who is undone by a trespassing dog pays $8. (b) Aurelio and the flier who is undone by a tangled line are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
9. The flier who flies a rokkaku fighter falls somewhere between Morwenna and the flier who uses parcel string in entry fee order.
10. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Ifeoma and the flier who uses kevlar thread are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other. (b) The flier who uses hemp cord is undone by a sudden lull.
11. Aurelio and the flier who uses kevlar thread are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
12. The flier who is undone by a lost tail has an entry fee exactly 2 times that of the flier who uses waxed cotton.
13. Blaise has an entry fee exactly $2 more than the flier who uses kevlar thread.
14. The flier who is undone by a snapped spar is cheaper than the flier who uses hemp cord in entry fee order.
15. The flier who flies a rokkaku fighter and the flier who uses hemp cord are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
16. The flier who flies a paper sled kite has an entry fee exactly $2 more than the flier who is undone by a trespassing dog.
17. Aurelio is cheaper than the flier who is undone by a trespassing dog in entry fee order.
18. The flier who uses nylon twine and the flier who is undone by a tangled line are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
19. The flier who flies a box kite is exactly one fee step cheaper than the flier who is undone by a lost tail, with no entry fee in between.
20. The flier who flies a hand-painted dragon is cheaper than the flier who uses nylon twine in entry fee order.
