# The Wind Is Free, the Field Is Not

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 6 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 16 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `5028cbce60ba`

## Introduction

The Dunnage Kite Festival charges by the kite, which has produced some creative definitions of 'one kite'.
Six fliers are involved. Determine each flier's entry fee, kite, mishap and line.
- **Flier**: Blaise, Morwenna, Ifeoma, Aurelio, Kipling and Gunnar.
- **Entry fee** (listed from cheaper to dearer): $2, $4, $6, $8, $10 and $12.
- **Kite**: a paper sled kite, a box kite, a hand-painted dragon, a delta wing, a seven-metre centipede and a rokkaku fighter.
- **Mishap**: a lost tail, a snapped spar, a tangled line, a sudden lull, a trespassing dog and a tree.
- **Line**: fishing monofilament, parcel string, hemp cord, waxed cotton, nylon twine and kevlar thread.
Every flier has a different entry fee, kite, mishap and line, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The flier who uses nylon twine is cheaper than $10 in entry fee order. $10 has an entry fee exactly $4 more than Morwenna. The flier who uses nylon twine does not pay $6.
2. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The flier who is undone by a tree is dearer than $6 in entry fee order. (b) The flier who uses waxed cotton pays $10.
3. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The flier who flies a hand-painted dragon and $12 are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other. (b) The flier who uses waxed cotton is cheaper than the flier who flies a hand-painted dragon in entry fee order.
4. $4 is exactly one fee step cheaper than the flier who flies a hand-painted dragon, with no entry fee in between.
5. Ifeoma does not pay $2.
6. The flier who flies a delta wing is exactly one fee step cheaper than $6, with no entry fee in between.
7. The following two do NOT both hold; either one alone may hold, and so may neither: (a) Aurelio and the flier who is undone by a lost tail are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other. (b) The flier who uses kevlar thread is dearer than $2 in entry fee order.
8. $6 falls somewhere between the flier who flies a rokkaku fighter and the flier who uses fishing monofilament in entry fee order.
9. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Blaise is dearer than $10 in entry fee order. (b) The flier who uses hemp cord is cheaper than Kipling in entry fee order.
10. The flier who is undone by a tangled line is exactly one fee step cheaper than Blaise, with no entry fee in between.
11. The flier who is undone by a tree has an entry fee exactly 3 times that of the flier who uses fishing monofilament.
12. The flier who flies a paper sled kite and the flier who is undone by a trespassing dog are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
13. Aurelio does not fly a paper sled kite.
14. Blaise is exactly one fee step cheaper than the flier who is undone by a sudden lull, with no entry fee in between.
15. The flier who flies a seven-metre centipede has an entry fee exactly $10 more than the flier who is undone by a snapped spar.
16. The following is NOT true: The flier who is undone by a trespassing dog is cheaper than the flier who uses kevlar thread in entry fee order.
