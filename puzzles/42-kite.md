# The Wind Is Free, the Field Is Not

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 13 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `02e1b932e31f`

## Introduction

The Dunnage Kite Festival charges by the kite, which has produced some creative definitions of 'one kite'.
Five fliers are involved. Determine each flier's entry fee, kite, mishap and line.
- **Flier**: Tansy, Blaise, Kipling, Ifeoma and Morwenna.
- **Entry fee** (listed from cheaper to dearer): $2, $4, $6, $8 and $10.
- **Kite**: a paper sled kite, a delta wing, a hand-painted dragon, a parafoil and a box kite.
- **Mishap**: a lost tail, a trespassing dog, a power line scare, a sudden lull and a tangled line.
- **Line**: kevlar thread, waxed cotton, hemp cord, parcel string and nylon twine.
Every flier has a different entry fee, kite, mishap and line, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The flier who is undone by a trespassing dog falls somewhere between $4 and the flier who uses hemp cord in entry fee order. The flier who is undone by a sudden lull does not pay $4.
2. The flier who flies a box kite and $2 are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
3. Blaise and the flier who is undone by a lost tail are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other. Blaise is dearer than $6 in entry fee order.
4. Morwenna is exactly one fee step cheaper than $10, with no entry fee in between.
5. Kipling and $8 are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
6. The flier who uses waxed cotton is cheaper than $6 in entry fee order.
7. Tansy falls somewhere between the flier who is undone by a lost tail and the flier who is undone by a power line scare in entry fee order.
8. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Ifeoma is cheaper than the flier who flies a hand-painted dragon in entry fee order. (b) Morwenna flies a paper sled kite.
9. The flier who uses nylon twine and the flier who is undone by a trespassing dog are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
10. The flier who is undone by a trespassing dog falls somewhere between Ifeoma and the flier who flies a delta wing in entry fee order.
11. The flier who flies a paper sled kite does not use parcel string.
12. The flier who flies a paper sled kite does not use waxed cotton.
13. The flier who flies a parafoil and the flier who is undone by a trespassing dog are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
