# The Hollow Creek Pie Judging

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 6 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 18 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `8ecb8f012128`

## Introduction

The Hollow Creek county fair runs its pie judging with more ceremony than the state legislature.
Six bakers are involved. Determine each baker's placing, filling, county and crust.
- **Baker**: Winnie, Bramwell, Caspian, Marigold, Odette and Thaddeus.
- **Placing** (listed from better to worse): first, second, third, fourth, fifth and sixth.
- **Filling**: pecan, green tomato, rhubarb, sour cherry, buttermilk and mincemeat.
- **Home county**: Dunmore, Quarry Hill, Ardmore, Sweetwater, Little Pell and Fenwick.
- **Crust**: a fluted rim, a fork-crimped edge, a cut-out star top, a braided edge, a double crust and a crumb topping.
Every baker has a different placing, filling, county and crust, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) The baker who uses a fluted rim is worse than second in placing order. (b) The baker who uses a cut-out star top is worse than fifth in placing order.
2. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The baker who uses a fork-crimped edge and the baker who bakes sour cherry are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other. (b) The baker who uses a double crust and second are exactly four places apart in placing order.
3. The baker who bakes sour cherry and fifth are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other.
4. Second is exactly one place better than Thaddeus, with no placing in between.
5. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The baker who bakes pecan is better than the baker who comes from Sweetwater in placing order. (b) The baker who comes from Fenwick and sixth both sit at odd-numbered places or both at even-numbered places.
6. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The baker who comes from Ardmore is worse than first in placing order. (b) The baker who uses a fork-crimped edge bakes buttermilk.
7. The baker who comes from Sweetwater and sixth are exactly three places apart in placing order.
8. The baker who bakes mincemeat and third are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other.
9. The baker who comes from Little Pell is better than Winnie in placing order.
10. Bramwell is better than the baker who bakes pecan in placing order.
11. Bramwell does not bake buttermilk.
12. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The baker who uses a double crust and the baker who bakes buttermilk are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other. (b) Odette is better than the baker who bakes green tomato in placing order.
13. The baker who uses a cut-out star top falls somewhere between the baker who bakes mincemeat and the baker who bakes rhubarb in placing order.
14. Marigold falls somewhere between the baker who comes from Little Pell and the baker who bakes pecan in placing order. The baker who comes from Little Pell does not use a crumb topping.
15. Caspian and the baker who uses a braided edge are exactly four places apart in placing order. Caspian and the baker who uses a cut-out star top are exactly two places apart in placing order.
16. Winnie and the baker who uses a crumb topping are exactly five places apart in placing order.
17. The baker who comes from Dunmore falls somewhere between Bramwell and Thaddeus in placing order.
18. One of Odette and the baker who bakes green tomato sits at an odd-numbered placing and the other at an even-numbered placing.
