# The Orchid House Reckoning

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 12 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `350cd7490211`

## Introduction

The orchid house at Fenn Park is shared by its growers, each convinced the others are overwatering.
Five growers are involved. Determine each grower's harvest month, orchid, potting mix and pest.
- **Grower**: Nkiruka, Ambrose, Columbine, Rowena and Taavi.
- **Harvest month** (listed from earlier to later): January, February, March, April and May.
- **Orchid**: a slipper orchid, a dancing lady, a vanilla vine, a jewel orchid and a moth orchid.
- **Potting mix**: bark chip; perlite and charcoal; coconut husk; tree fern fibre; and sphagnum moss.
- **Pest**: mealybug, scale insects, thrips, fungus gnats and root rot.
Every grower has a different harvest month, orchid, potting mix and pest, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Rowena and the grower who pots in sphagnum moss are exactly two months apart in harvest month order. (b) The grower who grows a vanilla vine harvests in February.
2. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The grower who grows a jewel orchid is later than May in harvest month order. (b) Columbine pots in tree fern fibre.
3. Ambrose is exactly one month earlier than March, with no harvest month in between.
4. The grower who is troubled by thrips and January are exactly one month apart in harvest month order, in one direction or the other.
5. April is exactly one month earlier than the grower who is troubled by fungus gnats, with no harvest month in between.
6. The grower who grows a slipper orchid and January are exactly one month apart in harvest month order, in one direction or the other.
7. The grower who grows a vanilla vine and the grower who is troubled by thrips are exactly one month apart in harvest month order, in one direction or the other.
8. The grower who pots in coconut husk and the grower who is troubled by root rot are exactly two months apart in harvest month order.
9. The grower who grows a moth orchid is earlier than the grower who pots in sphagnum moss in harvest month order.
10. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The grower who pots in bark chip and the grower who grows a dancing lady are exactly three months apart in harvest month order. (b) The grower who grows a slipper orchid is troubled by fungus gnats.
11. Nkiruka and the grower who grows a vanilla vine are exactly one month apart in harvest month order, in one direction or the other.
12. The grower who pots in coconut husk and the grower who is troubled by scale insects are exactly four months apart in harvest month order.
