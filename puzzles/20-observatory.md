# A Bad Week at the Pinhole Observatory

**Difficulty** 4/7 (moderate) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 15 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `2db0c99d717a`

## Introduction

The Pinhole Amateur Astronomical Society has one good telescope and a great many strong opinions about who may point it where.
Five astronomers are involved. Determine each astronomer's day, target, mishap and thermos.
- **Astronomer**: Beatrix, Sunniva, Ptolemy, Cornelius and Dmitri.
- **Day** (listed from earlier to later): Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday.
- **Observed**: the Andromeda galaxy, a variable star, the Crab Nebula, a comet fragment and Saturn's rings.
- **Mishap**: a neighbour's floodlight, a flat battery, a seized mount, an unexpected cloud bank and a dewed-up eyepiece.
- **Thermos contents**: barley water, black coffee, stewed tea, mulled cider and oxtail soup.
Every astronomer has a different day, target, mishap and thermos, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The astronomer who brings barley water observes on Friday.
2. Tuesday falls somewhere between the astronomer who brings oxtail soup and the astronomer who brings stewed tea in day order. Tuesday falls somewhere between Ptolemy and Sunniva in day order.
3. The astronomer who is undone by a flat battery is later than Wednesday in day order.
4. Wednesday is exactly one day earlier than the astronomer who studies the Andromeda galaxy, with no day in between.
5. The astronomer who is undone by a seized mount and Tuesday are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other.
6. Tuesday falls somewhere between the astronomer who is undone by a dewed-up eyepiece and the astronomer who is undone by a flat battery in day order.
7. The astronomer who studies the Crab Nebula does not observe on Monday.
8. The astronomer who brings oxtail soup is exactly one day earlier than Dmitri, with no day in between. Thursday falls somewhere between Beatrix and Dmitri in day order.
9. The astronomer who is undone by an unexpected cloud bank and the astronomer who brings oxtail soup are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other.
10. Sunniva and the astronomer who studies Saturn's rings are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other.
11. The astronomer who is undone by a neighbour's floodlight is earlier than Beatrix in day order.
12. Ptolemy does not bring oxtail soup.
13. The astronomer who is undone by a neighbour's floodlight and the astronomer who studies a comet fragment are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other.
14. The astronomer who brings black coffee and the astronomer who studies the Andromeda galaxy are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other.
15. The astronomer who brings oxtail soup and the astronomer who studies a comet fragment are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other.
