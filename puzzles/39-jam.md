# Preserves, and the Preservation of Grudges

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 13 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `90152f81ece0`

## Introduction

The preserves tent is the most competitive building at the show, and the only one with a locked door.
Five entrants are involved. Determine each entrant's price, preserve, jar and ingredient.
- **Entrant**: Ottilie, Emeka, Drusilla, Rafferty and Stanislaw.
- **Price** (listed from cheaper to dearer): $3, $5, $7, $9 and $11.
- **Preserve**: sloe and apple butter; damson cheese; hot pepper jelly; green walnut pickle; and medlar jam.
- **Jar**: an unlabelled jar, a jar with a cloth lid, a recycled honey jar, a sealed crock and a hexagonal jar.
- **Secret ingredient**: a bay leaf, orange zest, a splash of gin, nothing whatsoever and star anise.
Every entrant has a different price, preserve, jar and ingredient, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. Drusilla charges $7. The entrant who enters damson cheese is exactly one price step cheaper than $7, with no price in between.
2. The entrant who uses a recycled honey jar and $9 are exactly one price step apart in price order, in one direction or the other.
3. The entrant who adds nothing whatsoever and $9 are exactly one price step apart in price order, in one direction or the other.
4. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The entrant who adds star anise charges $7. (b) Emeka does not enter medlar jam.
5. The entrant who enters sloe and apple butter and $9 are exactly one price step apart in price order, in one direction or the other.
6. Rafferty and the entrant who enters green walnut pickle are exactly one price step apart in price order, in one direction or the other.
7. The entrant who enters green walnut pickle is exactly one price step cheaper than the entrant who uses a hexagonal jar, with no price in between.
8. The following two do NOT both hold; either one alone may hold, and so may neither: (a) The entrant who adds star anise is exactly one price step cheaper than the entrant who uses a recycled honey jar, with no price in between. (b) Ottilie does not add orange zest.
9. The entrant who adds a splash of gin is exactly one price step cheaper than the entrant who enters green walnut pickle, with no price in between.
10. The entrant who enters sloe and apple butter is cheaper than the entrant who uses an unlabelled jar in price order.
11. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Rafferty and the entrant who adds a splash of gin are exactly one price step apart in price order, in one direction or the other. (b) The entrant who enters medlar jam adds a bay leaf.
12. Emeka is cheaper than the entrant who adds orange zest in price order.
13. The entrant who uses a sealed crock and the entrant who enters medlar jam are exactly one price step apart in price order, in one direction or the other.
