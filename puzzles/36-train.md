# The Loft Layout Dispute

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 14 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `106ef4f68529`

## Introduction

The members of the model railway society share one loft layout and a deep well of resentment.
Five modellers are involved. Determine each modeller's shelf, stock, scenery and grievance.
- **Modeller**: Rosamond, Cuthbert, Gilbert, Lavinia and Esperanza.
- **Shelf** (listed from lower to higher): the bottom shelf, the second shelf, the third shelf, the fourth shelf and the fifth shelf.
- **Rolling stock**: a scratch-built crane wagon, a brass pannier tank, a kit-built brake van, a rake of milk tankers and an unpainted shunter.
- **Scenery**: a river of resin, a plaster tunnel mouth, a working level crossing, a flocked cutting and a sheet of static grass.
- **Grievance**: the lighting, a missing screwdriver, the wiring, the position of the fiddle yard and the choice of era.
Every modeller has a different shelf, stock, scenery and grievance, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The modeller who builds a sheet of static grass owns a brass pannier tank.
2. The modeller who complains about the lighting is exactly one shelf lower than the second shelf, with no shelf in between.
3. The modeller who complains about the position of the fiddle yard is exactly one shelf lower than the fourth shelf, with no shelf in between.
4. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The modeller who owns a rake of milk tankers is lower than the modeller who complains about the lighting in shelf order. (b) The bottom shelf and the modeller who owns a kit-built brake van both sit at odd-numbered shelves or both at even-numbered shelves.
5. The second shelf is exactly one shelf lower than the modeller who builds a working level crossing, with no shelf in between.
6. The modeller who owns a rake of milk tankers is exactly one shelf lower than the fourth shelf, with no shelf in between.
7. Esperanza is higher than the third shelf in shelf order.
8. The modeller who owns an unpainted shunter is lower than Cuthbert in shelf order.
9. The modeller who owns a kit-built brake van is lower than the modeller who complains about a missing screwdriver in shelf order.
10. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The modeller who complains about the wiring is lower than Gilbert in shelf order. (b) Cuthbert is lower than the modeller who complains about the wiring in shelf order.
11. Rosamond does not build a river of resin.
12. Rosamond does not complain about the wiring.
13. The modeller who builds a sheet of static grass falls somewhere between the modeller who complains about the lighting and the modeller who complains about the position of the fiddle yard in shelf order.
14. The modeller who complains about the choice of era falls somewhere between the modeller who builds a plaster tunnel mouth and the modeller who builds a river of resin in shelf order.
