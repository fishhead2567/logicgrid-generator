# Andy's Swiftest Birds

**Difficulty** 6/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 16 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `29e9aa571c23`

## Introduction

Andy races pigeons, and each bird is bred for a different combination of stubbornness and speed.
Five pigeons are involved. Determine each pigeon's distance, plumage, breed and loft.
- **Pigeon**: Dot, Pip, Nutmeg, Alabaster and Grimsby.
- **Distance** (listed from shorter to longer): 200 miles, 300 miles, 400 miles, 500 miles and 600 miles.
- **Plumage**: grizzle, mealy red, slate blue, chequered black and pied white.
- **Breed**: a Stichelbaut, a Van Loon, a Sion, a Janssen and a Busschaert.
- **Loft**: the gable loft, the stable loft, the orchard loft, the garage loft and the chapel loft.
Every pigeon has a different distance, plumage, breed and loft, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The pigeon that descends from a Janssen flies 500 miles. (b) The pigeon that is chequered black does not fly 300 miles.
2. The pigeon that is kept in the chapel loft is exactly one hundred miles shorter than 500 miles, with no distance in between.
3. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Dot flies 300 miles. (b) Grimsby is not kept in the chapel loft.
4. The pigeon that is mealy red falls somewhere between the pigeon that descends from a Janssen and 500 miles in distance order.
5. The pigeon that is kept in the stable loft and 300 miles are exactly one hundred miles apart in distance order, in one direction or the other.
6. Grimsby and the pigeon that descends from a Sion are exactly one hundred miles apart in distance order, in one direction or the other.
7. The pigeon that is kept in the garage loft is exactly one hundred miles shorter than the pigeon that is pied white, with no distance in between.
8. Alabaster does not descend from a Van Loon.
9. Grimsby is shorter than the pigeon that is kept in the garage loft in distance order.
10. The pigeon that descends from a Van Loon is not chequered black.
11. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) Grimsby is not kept in the chapel loft. (b) The pigeon that descends from a Janssen is kept in the chapel loft.
12. Pip is shorter than the pigeon that is kept in the gable loft in distance order.
13. The pigeon that descends from a Sion is not slate blue.
14. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The pigeon that descends from a Janssen and the pigeon that is kept in the chapel loft are exactly one hundred miles apart in distance order, in one direction or the other. (b) The pigeon that descends from a Stichelbaut and the pigeon that is kept in the chapel loft are exactly one hundred miles apart in distance order, in one direction or the other.
15. Pip and the pigeon that is chequered black are exactly one hundred miles apart in distance order, in one direction or the other.
16. The pigeon that is grizzle is exactly one hundred miles shorter than Dot, with no distance in between.
