# The Honey Show Scandal

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 13 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `1fd32b0c67ff`

## Introduction

The annual honey show has been judged, and at least two people are writing letters about it.
Five beekeepers are involved. Determine each beekeeper's entry fee, honey, hive and note.
- **Beekeeper**: Antonina, Wilhelmina, Mungo, Fausto and Ezekiel.
- **Entry fee** (listed from cheaper to dearer): $2, $4, $6, $8 and $10.
- **Honey**: sea lavender honey, ivy honey, clover honey, heather honey and lime blossom honey.
- **Hive type**: a top-bar hive, a Warre hive, a long deep hive, a skep and a national hive.
- **Judge's note**: an unlabelled lid, exemplary clarity, slightly over-filtered, a cloudy jar and an overbearing aroma.
Every beekeeper has a different entry fee, honey, hive and note, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The beekeeper who keeps a national hive pays $6. (b) The beekeeper who has a jar noted as an unlabelled lid does not pay $2.
2. Antonina falls somewhere between $4 and $8 in entry fee order.
3. $8 falls somewhere between the beekeeper who keeps a long deep hive and the beekeeper who has a jar noted as an unlabelled lid in entry fee order.
4. The beekeeper who enters sea lavender honey has not a jar noted as slightly over-filtered. The beekeeper who has a jar noted as slightly over-filtered does not pay $4.
5. $4 falls somewhere between the beekeeper who keeps a Warre hive and the beekeeper who enters clover honey in entry fee order.
6. The beekeeper who enters ivy honey is exactly one fee step cheaper than $6, with no entry fee in between.
7. The beekeeper who enters clover honey and $10 are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
8. The beekeeper who keeps a national hive and the beekeeper who has a jar noted as exemplary clarity are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
9. Wilhelmina falls somewhere between the beekeeper who enters clover honey and the beekeeper who has a jar noted as an unlabelled lid in entry fee order.
10. The beekeeper who keeps a skep and the beekeeper who has a jar noted as an overbearing aroma are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
11. The beekeeper who keeps a skep is exactly one fee step cheaper than the beekeeper who enters lime blossom honey, with no entry fee in between.
12. The beekeeper who keeps a national hive is cheaper than Fausto in entry fee order.
13. Ezekiel is cheaper than the beekeeper who enters sea lavender honey in entry fee order. Ezekiel does not keep a Warre hive.
