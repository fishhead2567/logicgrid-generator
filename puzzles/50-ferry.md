# The Crossings to Inverlaig

**Difficulty** 6/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 6 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 17 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `cf20524e8300`

## Introduction

The Inverlaig ferry runs a full day of crossings in theory, and a variable number in practice.
Six skippers are involved. Determine each skipper's start time, cargo, vessel and reason.
- **Skipper**: Murdo, Ruaridh, Eilidh, Senga, Calla and Tormod.
- **Start time** (listed from earlier to later): 8am, 9am, 10am, 11am, noon and 1pm.
- **Notable cargo**: a brass band; nineteen sheep; a refrigerated van; a wedding party; a consignment of bees; and a film crew and no permits.
- **Vessel**: the Broch Maid, the Ailsa Belle, the Kelpie, the Siller Grey, the Tern and the Steadfast.
- **Delay blamed on**: a slipway repair, fog, the tide, a missing deckhand, paperwork and a fouled propeller.
Every skipper has a different start time, cargo, vessel and reason, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) The skipper who blames the tide sails at 1pm. (b) The skipper who blames a fouled propeller does not sail at 8am.
2. The skipper who commands the Broch Maid and 1pm are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other.
3. The skipper who commands the Kelpie and 10am are exactly two hours apart in start time order.
4. The skipper who commands the Ailsa Belle falls somewhere between the skipper who carries a brass band and 1pm in start time order.
5. Ruaridh is earlier than noon in start time order.
6. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Murdo and 9am are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other. (b) The skipper who carries a consignment of bees is earlier than Calla in start time order.
7. The skipper who blames a fouled propeller and 9am are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other.
8. The skipper who blames the tide and 1pm both sit at odd-numbered hours or both at even-numbered hours.
9. The skipper who carries a film crew and no permits falls somewhere between the skipper who blames a slipway repair and 1pm in start time order.
10. 9am is exactly one hour earlier than the skipper who carries a film crew and no permits, with no start time in between.
11. The skipper who blames a missing deckhand and Ruaridh are exactly four hours apart in start time order.
12. The skipper who carries a wedding party and the skipper who commands the Broch Maid are exactly three hours apart in start time order.
13. The skipper who blames a missing deckhand is exactly one hour earlier than Murdo, with no start time in between.
14. The skipper who carries a refrigerated van and the skipper who blames paperwork are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other.
15. Tormod falls somewhere between the skipper who carries nineteen sheep and the skipper who commands the Steadfast in start time order.
16. The skipper who blames a fouled propeller is exactly one hour earlier than the skipper who commands the Tern, with no start time in between.
17. The skipper who commands the Tern is earlier than Eilidh in start time order.
