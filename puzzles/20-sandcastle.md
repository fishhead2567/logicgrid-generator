## Introduction

The Grindle Bay Open is judged at four o'clock, whatever the tide is doing.
Five builders are involved. Determine each builder's placing, build, tool and cause.
- **Builder**: Khalid, Fenwick, Hartley, Aleksy and Bronagh.
- **Placing** (listed from better to worse): first, second, third, fourth and fifth.
- **Build**: a moated keep, a sleeping dragon, a cathedral with flying buttresses, a scale model of the pier and a spiral ziggurat.
- **Chief tool**: a pastry brush, a soup spoon, bare hands, a child's rake and a bucket with no handle.
- **Undone by**: a cricket ball, structural overreach, a sudden shower, a gull and a loose dog.
Every builder has a different placing, build, tool and cause, so within each category every item is used exactly once.
