# The Wind Is Free, the Field Is Not

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 13 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `85f8bfcd8a78`

## Introduction

The Dunnage Kite Festival charges by the kite, which has produced some creative definitions of 'one kite'.
Five fliers are involved. Determine each flier's entry fee, kite, mishap and line.
- **Flier**: Kipling, Aurelio, Blaise, Ifeoma and Tansy.
- **Entry fee** (listed from cheaper to dearer): $2, $4, $6, $8 and $10.
- **Kite**: a hand-painted dragon, a rokkaku fighter, a box kite, a paper sled kite and a seven-metre centipede.
- **Mishap**: a sudden lull, a tangled line, a lost tail, a power line scare and a trespassing dog.
- **Line**: fishing monofilament, braided dacron, nylon twine, hemp cord and kevlar thread.
Every flier has a different entry fee, kite, mishap and line, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The flier who is undone by a power line scare pays $2.
2. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Blaise pays $2. (b) Tansy pays $4.
3. The flier who flies a paper sled kite and $10 are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
4. The flier who uses fishing monofilament falls somewhere between $10 and the flier who is undone by a lost tail in entry fee order.
5. The flier who flies a rokkaku fighter and the flier who uses hemp cord are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
6. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Tansy and the flier who flies a paper sled kite are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other. (b) Tansy does not use hemp cord.
7. Blaise is exactly one fee step cheaper than the flier who flies a hand-painted dragon, with no entry fee in between.
8. The flier who is undone by a trespassing dog falls somewhere between the flier who flies a rokkaku fighter and the flier who uses braided dacron in entry fee order.
9. Aurelio is exactly one fee step cheaper than the flier who is undone by a lost tail, with no entry fee in between.
10. The flier who flies a seven-metre centipede and the flier who is undone by a trespassing dog are exactly one fee step apart in entry fee order, in one direction or the other.
11. Blaise is exactly one fee step cheaper than the flier who is undone by a sudden lull, with no entry fee in between.
12. The flier who uses kevlar thread falls somewhere between Kipling and Tansy in entry fee order.
13. The flier who uses kevlar thread is exactly one fee step cheaper than Blaise, with no entry fee in between.
