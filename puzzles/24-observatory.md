# A Bad Week at the Pinhole Observatory

*The Pinhole Amateur Astronomical Society has one good telescope and a great many strong opinions about who may point it where.*

## Introduction

There are 5 sessions. Each has exactly one day and one item from every other category below, and within each category every item belongs to a different session.
- **Day** (listed in order): Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday.
- **Observed**: Saturn's rings, a variable star, the Andromeda galaxy, a lunar occultation and a comet fragment.
- **Mishap**: a seized mount, a flat battery, a dropped lens cap, a neighbour's floodlight and an unexpected cloud bank.
- **Thermos contents**: barley water, bone broth, cocoa, oxtail soup and stewed tea.
The days above are listed in order, from Monday to Friday.

## Clues

1. Bone broth goes with a lunar occultation.
2. Stewed tea and a comet fragment both sit at odd-numbered days or both at even-numbered days. A seized mount and a comet fragment are next to each other in day order, in one order or the other. Oxtail soup goes with a comet fragment.
3. Monday is immediately earlier than a flat battery's day, with no day in between. A flat battery and a variable star are next to each other in day order, in one order or the other.
4. Wednesday is immediately earlier than a dropped lens cap's day, with no day in between.
5. Saturn's rings' day is immediately earlier than a dropped lens cap's day, with no day in between.
6. An unexpected cloud bank and barley water are exactly four days apart in day order.
7. A variable star's day is earlier than a neighbour's floodlight's day.
