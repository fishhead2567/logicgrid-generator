# A Fortnight at the Little Vesper Theatre

**Difficulty** 5/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 15 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `ebe66b5bd069`

## Introduction

The Little Vesper Theatre seats forty people and employs a company of puppeteers who will not be in the same room together.
Five puppeteers are involved. Determine each puppeteer's slot, puppet, play and accompaniment.
- **Puppeteer**: Josefa, Bowen, Halima, Lorcan and Stellan.
- **Running order** (listed from earlier to later): slot one, slot two, slot three, slot four and slot five.
- **Lead puppet**: a paper crane, a tin soldier, a shepherd boy, a one-eyed cat and a moth queen.
- **Play**: Hollowmas, The Clockwork Orchard, Winterling, The Drowned Bell and The Glass Tide.
- **Accompaniment**: a hurdy-gurdy, a bowed psaltery, a musical saw, a pump organ and a glass harmonica.
Every puppeteer has a different slot, puppet, play and accompaniment, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The puppeteer who works a one-eyed cat performs in slot two.
2. The puppeteer who stages The Glass Tide falls somewhere between the puppeteer who works a shepherd boy and slot five in slot order.
3. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The puppeteer who stages Hollowmas and the puppeteer who works a shepherd boy are exactly two slots apart in slot order. (b) The puppeteer who works a moth queen performs in slot four.
4. The puppeteer who stages The Drowned Bell and slot five are exactly one slot apart in slot order, in one direction or the other.
5. Bowen and slot one are exactly two slots apart in slot order.
6. The puppeteer who is accompanied by a hurdy-gurdy and slot four are exactly one slot apart in slot order, in one direction or the other.
7. One of the puppeteer who is accompanied by a musical saw and slot three sits at an odd-numbered slot and the other at an even-numbered slot.
8. The puppeteer who stages The Drowned Bell and Stellan both sit at odd-numbered slots or both at even-numbered slots.
9. The puppeteer who is accompanied by a hurdy-gurdy and the puppeteer who works a moth queen are exactly two slots apart in slot order.
10. The puppeteer who works a shepherd boy is earlier than the puppeteer who is accompanied by a glass harmonica in slot order.
11. The puppeteer who works a paper crane is earlier than the puppeteer who stages Winterling in slot order.
12. The puppeteer who works a moth queen falls somewhere between the puppeteer who is accompanied by a pump organ and Lorcan in slot order.
13. The puppeteer who is accompanied by a pump organ and the puppeteer who stages Hollowmas are exactly one slot apart in slot order, in one direction or the other.
14. The puppeteer who is accompanied by a musical saw does not stage The Glass Tide.
15. Josefa is exactly one slot earlier than the puppeteer who stages Winterling, with no slot in between.
