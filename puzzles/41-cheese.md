# The Cheesemongers' Standoff

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 13 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `107e997e8942`

## Introduction

A case of opinionated cheeses, their equally opinionated makers, and one display that has begun to smell like a political scandal.
Five cheeses are involved. Determine each cheese's price, maker, rind and ageing.
- **Cheese**: Saltpans, Old Thrashing, Glebe Farmhouse, Winter Tarn and Oakmoss.
- **Price** (listed from cheaper to dearer): $3, $5, $7, $9 and $11.
- **Maker**: Lachlan, Olubunmi, Hepzibah, Sigrid and Perdita.
- **Rind**: a cloth-bound rind, an ash-coated rind, a nettle-wrapped rind, a cider-washed rind and a washed rind.
- **Aged for**: nine months, two months, three weeks, five months and fourteen months.
Every cheese has a different price, maker, rind and ageing, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The cheese that was made by Sigrid sells for $3. (b) The cheese that has an ash-coated rind does not sell for $9.
2. $5 is exactly one price step cheaper than the cheese that was made by Sigrid, with no price in between.
3. Glebe Farmhouse and $7 are exactly one price step apart in price order, in one direction or the other.
4. Saltpans is dearer than $9 in price order.
5. The cheese that has a cloth-bound rind falls somewhere between the cheese that was made by Perdita and $3 in price order.
6. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The cheese that has an ash-coated rind is exactly one price step cheaper than the cheese that was aged for five months, with no price in between. (b) The cheese that has an ash-coated rind sells for $9.
7. The cheese that was aged for fourteen months and the cheese that was made by Hepzibah are exactly one price step apart in price order, in one direction or the other.
8. Winter Tarn falls somewhere between the cheese that has a cloth-bound rind and the cheese that has a nettle-wrapped rind in price order.
9. The cheese that was made by Perdita is cheaper than the cheese that has a washed rind in price order.
10. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The cheese that has a nettle-wrapped rind is exactly one price step cheaper than the cheese that was aged for nine months, with no price in between. (b) The cheese that was aged for five months does not have a cloth-bound rind.
11. The cheese that was aged for two months is exactly one price step cheaper than the cheese that was made by Olubunmi, with no price in between.
12. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The cheese that was aged for fourteen months and the cheese that was made by Perdita are exactly one price step apart in price order, in one direction or the other. (b) The cheese that was aged for fourteen months is cheaper than Oakmoss in price order.
13. The cheese that has a nettle-wrapped rind is cheaper than the cheese that was aged for two months in price order.
