# Andy's Swiftest Birds

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 6 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 15 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `7fc95bad5a8e`

## Introduction

Andy races pigeons, and each bird is bred for a different combination of stubbornness and speed.
Six pigeons are involved. Determine each pigeon's distance, plumage, breed and loft.
- **Pigeon**: Grimsby, Pip, Hieronymus, Dot, Nutmeg and Cadwallader.
- **Distance** (listed from shorter to longer): 200 miles, 300 miles, 400 miles, 500 miles, 600 miles and 700 miles.
- **Plumage**: slate blue, bronze, chequered black, grizzle, pied white and dun.
- **Breed**: a Sion, a Busschaert, a Stichelbaut, a Delbar, a Janssen and a Van Loon.
- **Loft**: the canal loft, the stable loft, the garage loft, the gable loft, the chapel loft and the allotment loft.
Every pigeon has a different distance, plumage, breed and loft, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The pigeon that is pied white and 600 miles are exactly one hundred miles apart in distance order, in one direction or the other. (b) The pigeon that is dun flies 300 miles.
2. The following two do NOT both hold; either one alone may hold, and so may neither: (a) The pigeon that is slate blue is longer than 500 miles in distance order. (b) The pigeon that descends from a Sion flies 400 miles.
3. 600 miles is exactly one hundred miles shorter than the pigeon that descends from a Janssen, with no distance in between.
4. 500 miles falls somewhere between Grimsby and Nutmeg in distance order.
5. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The pigeon that is kept in the allotment loft and 600 miles are exactly one hundred miles apart in distance order, in one direction or the other. (b) The pigeon that descends from a Delbar flies 600 miles.
6. The pigeon that is grizzle has a distance exactly 100 miles more than the pigeon that descends from a Stichelbaut.
7. The pigeon that is chequered black is shorter than the pigeon that is kept in the chapel loft in distance order.
8. The pigeon that descends from a Janssen has a distance exactly 100 miles more than the pigeon that is kept in the allotment loft.
9. The pigeon that descends from a Busschaert is exactly one hundred miles shorter than the pigeon that is kept in the garage loft, with no distance in between.
10. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) Nutmeg does not descend from a Busschaert. (b) Cadwallader is kept in the canal loft.
11. Pip is exactly one hundred miles shorter than the pigeon that descends from a Sion, with no distance in between.
12. Nutmeg falls somewhere between the pigeon that is pied white and the pigeon that is slate blue in distance order.
13. The pigeon that descends from a Stichelbaut is exactly one hundred miles shorter than Dot, with no distance in between.
14. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) Nutmeg does not descend from a Busschaert. (b) The pigeon that is kept in the gable loft is exactly one hundred miles shorter than Pip, with no distance in between.
15. The pigeon that is pied white is exactly one hundred miles shorter than the pigeon that is kept in the garage loft, with no distance in between.
