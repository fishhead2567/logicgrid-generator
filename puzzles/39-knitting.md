## Introduction

The Ashfold Knitting Circle meets weekly and has not agreed on anything since 1998.
Five knitters are involved. Determine each knitter's day, project, yarn and setback.
- **Knitter**: Serafina, Nkechi, Wilhelm, Maudie and Ebba.
- **Day** (listed from earlier to later): Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday.
- **Project**: a shawl of impossible lace, a fisherman's gansey, a cable-knit blanket, a dog coat and a Fair Isle tam.
- **Yarn**: recycled sari silk, nettle fibre, mill-end acrylic, hand-spun alpaca and undyed Shetland.
- **Setback**: unexplained shrinkage, running out of dye lot, a cat, losing the pattern entirely and a misread chart.
Every knitter has a different day, project, yarn and setback, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

3. The knitter who is making a Fair Isle tam and Monday are exactly two days apart in day order.

5. The knitter who is making a cable-knit blanket and the knitter who works in nettle fibre are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other.
6. The knitter who is set back by a misread chart is earlier than the knitter who is making a Fair Isle tam in day order.
7. The knitter who is set back by a cat falls somewhere between Wilhelm and the knitter who works in nettle fibre in day order.
8. Ebba and the knitter who is making a Fair Isle tam are exactly one day apart in day order, in one direction or the other.
9. The knitter who is making a fisherman's gansey is earlier than the knitter who is set back by a misread chart in day order.
