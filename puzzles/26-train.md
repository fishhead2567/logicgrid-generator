# The Loft Layout Dispute

**Difficulty** 3/7 (moderate) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 4 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 11 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `f4c874a2d202`

## Introduction

The members of the model railway society share one loft layout and a deep well of resentment.
Five modellers are involved. Determine each modeller's shelf, stock and grievance.
- **Modeller**: Gilbert, Esperanza, Rosamond, Lavinia and Abiodun.
- **Shelf** (listed from lower to higher): the bottom shelf, the second shelf, the third shelf, the fourth shelf and the fifth shelf.
- **Rolling stock**: a kit-built brake van, a battered autocoach, a rake of milk tankers, a brass pannier tank and a gleaming Pacific.
- **Grievance**: somebody else's couplings, the choice of era, the position of the fiddle yard, the lighting and the timetable.
Every modeller has a different shelf, stock and grievance, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The modeller who complains about the lighting stores on the third shelf.
2. The modeller who complains about the choice of era stores on the fourth shelf.
3. The third shelf falls somewhere between the modeller who complains about somebody else's couplings and Lavinia in shelf order.
4. The modeller who owns a kit-built brake van and the fifth shelf are exactly three shelves apart in shelf order.
5. Esperanza is lower than the fifth shelf in shelf order.
6. The second shelf falls somewhere between the modeller who owns a battered autocoach and the modeller who owns a rake of milk tankers in shelf order.
7. The modeller who complains about the position of the fiddle yard and Rosamond are exactly two shelves apart in shelf order. Rosamond does not own a gleaming Pacific.
8. The modeller who complains about the position of the fiddle yard is lower than Gilbert in shelf order.
9. The modeller who complains about the timetable and the modeller who owns a battered autocoach are exactly four shelves apart in shelf order.
10. Abiodun and the modeller who owns a rake of milk tankers are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other.
11. The modeller who complains about the timetable and the modeller who owns a kit-built brake van are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other.
