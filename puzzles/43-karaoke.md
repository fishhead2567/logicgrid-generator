## Introduction

The Dented Bell runs karaoke on Tuesdays. Attendance is loyal; talent is not.
Five singers are involved. Determine each singer's slot, song, drink and reception.
- **Singer**: Mercy, Vikram, Bogdan, Caitriona and Annalise.
- **Running order** (listed from earlier to later): slot one, slot two, slot three, slot four and slot five.
- **Song**: a sea shanty, a power ballad, a rap performed entirely from memory, a disco standard and a maudlin folk song.
- **Drink**: a lime and soda; a double whisky; a rum and coke; a pint of mild; and a cider in a plastic cup.
- **Reception**: scattered applause, polite silence, one heckler, a singalong and a standing ovation.
Every singer has a different slot, song, drink and reception, so within each category every item is used exactly once.
