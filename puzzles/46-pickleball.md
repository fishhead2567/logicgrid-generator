# The Tuesday Pickleball Ladder

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 6 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 18 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `be1bf3602a2c`

## Introduction

The rec center runs a pickleball ladder on Tuesdays. Courts go by start time, and the sign-up sheet gets rewritten more than once.
Six players are involved. Determine each player's start time, paddle, serve and gripe.
- **Player**: Yolanda, Arlene, Ramesh, Pete Ng, Dot Feeney and Buzz Kovacs.
- **Start time** (listed from earlier to later): 8am, 9am, 10am, 11am, noon and 1pm.
- **Paddle**: a carbon-fiber paddle, a graphite paddle, a hand-me-down paddle, an oversize paddle, a wooden loaner and a bright orange paddle.
- **Serve**: a high lob, a flat drive, a spin serve, a sidearm toss, the same serve every time and a lefty slice.
- **Gripe**: a wet court, a bad knee, the sun, nobody at all, a dead ball and the noisy court next door.
Every player has a different start time, paddle, serve and gripe, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

9. Pete Ng falls somewhere between the player who plays with a wooden loaner and 9am in start time order.
10. The player who serves with a sidearm toss and 11am are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other.
11. Dot Feeney falls somewhere between the player who serves with a sidearm toss and the player who serves with the same serve every time in start time order.
12. The player who plays with a hand-me-down paddle is exactly one hour earlier than the player who serves with a lefty slice, with no start time in between.
13. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The player who plays with a bright orange paddle is exactly one hour earlier than the player who serves with a sidearm toss, with no start time in between. (b) Buzz Kovacs plays with a hand-me-down paddle.
14. The player who blames a wet court and the player who plays with an oversize paddle are exactly three hours apart in start time order.
15. The following is NOT true: The player who plays with a bright orange paddle is earlier than the player who serves with the same serve every time in start time order.
16. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Dot Feeney blames a wet court. (b) One of the player who blames the sun and the player who plays with a graphite paddle sits at an odd-numbered start time and the other at an even-numbered start time.
17. The player who blames a dead ball and the player who serves with a flat drive are exactly four hours apart in start time order.
18. Pete Ng is exactly one hour earlier than the player who blames the noisy court next door, with no start time in between.
