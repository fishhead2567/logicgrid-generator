# Plot Wars on the Ashcombe Allotments

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 13 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `86a9570178fb`

## Introduction

The Ashcombe allotment committee has been locked in a slow war over compost, shade and the correct way to stake a bean.
Five plot-holders are involved. Determine each plot-holder's placing, crop, plot and shed.
- **Plot-holder**: Ewan, Senga, Obadiah, Alphonse and Kasimir.
- **Placing** (listed from better to worse): first, second, third, fourth and fifth.
- **Prize crop**: a cauliflower, a runner bean, a row of leeks, a rhubarb crown and a marrow.
- **Plot**: plot 31, plot 11, plot 7, plot 2 and plot 19.
- **Shed**: a tar-paper shed, a corrugated lean-to, a greenhouse, an upturned boat and a brick bothy.
Every plot-holder has a different placing, crop, plot and shed, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The plot-holder who keeps an upturned boat and second are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other. (b) The plot-holder who works plot 31 and fourth are exactly two places apart in placing order.
2. One of Obadiah and second sits at an odd-numbered placing and the other at an even-numbered placing.
3. Kasimir and fourth are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other.
4. First is exactly one place better than the plot-holder who keeps an upturned boat, with no placing in between.
5. Second is exactly one place better than the plot-holder who keeps a tar-paper shed, with no placing in between.
6. The plot-holder who grows a marrow is better than fourth in placing order.
7. Ewan does not grow a rhubarb crown. The plot-holder who grows a cauliflower falls somewhere between Ewan and first in placing order.
8. The plot-holder who works plot 19 is worse than third in placing order. Senga falls somewhere between the plot-holder who works plot 19 and the plot-holder who works plot 2 in placing order.
9. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Senga and the plot-holder who keeps a corrugated lean-to are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other. (b) The plot-holder who grows a row of leeks and the plot-holder who keeps a brick bothy both sit at odd-numbered places or both at even-numbered places.
10. The plot-holder who grows a marrow and the plot-holder who keeps a brick bothy are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other.
11. The plot-holder who works plot 7 and the plot-holder who keeps a corrugated lean-to are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other.
12. The plot-holder who grows a runner bean and the plot-holder who works plot 31 are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other.
13. Obadiah and the plot-holder who keeps a greenhouse are exactly two places apart in placing order.
