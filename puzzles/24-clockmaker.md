# A Benchful of Clocks, and No Agreement

**Difficulty** 4/7 (moderate) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 13 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `a34157356adb`

## Introduction

Mr Halloran repairs clocks. Those on the bench are all insisting on a different hour.
Five clocks are involved. Determine each clock's shelf, owner, fault and chime.
- **Clock**: a carriage clock, a ship's chronometer, a station clock, an anniversary clock and a mantel clock under glass.
- **Shelf** (listed from lower to higher): the bottom shelf, the second shelf, the third shelf, the fourth shelf and the fifth shelf.
- **Owner**: Captain Mott, Ms Abernathy, Mr Dziedzic, Father Brennan and Miss Trevelyan.
- **Fault**: a worn escape wheel, a seized fusee, a cracked suspension spring, a missing click spring and a bent pendulum rod.
- **Chime**: no chime at all; Westminster; Whittington; a bell and gong; and a ship's bell.
Every clock has a different shelf, owner, fault and chime, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The clock that belongs to Ms Abernathy and the third shelf are exactly two shelves apart in shelf order.
2. A ship's chronometer and the fifth shelf are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other.
3. The clock that belongs to Mr Dziedzic falls somewhere between the clock that sounds Whittington and the bottom shelf in shelf order.
4. A carriage clock and the fifth shelf are exactly three shelves apart in shelf order.
5. The clock that suffers from a worn escape wheel is exactly one shelf lower than the clock that sounds Westminster, with no shelf in between.
6. The clock that suffers from a bent pendulum rod is exactly one shelf lower than the clock that sounds a bell and gong, with no shelf in between.
7. The clock that suffers from a cracked suspension spring is lower than an anniversary clock in shelf order.
8. The clock that sounds a bell and gong and the clock that belongs to Father Brennan are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other.
9. The clock that sounds no chime at all is exactly one shelf lower than the clock that belongs to Ms Abernathy, with no shelf in between.
10. The clock that belongs to Captain Mott is exactly one shelf lower than an anniversary clock, with no shelf in between.
11. The clock that sounds Westminster and the clock that suffers from a missing click spring are exactly two shelves apart in shelf order.
12. The clock that sounds a ship's bell does not suffer from a bent pendulum rod.
13. A station clock and the clock that belongs to Captain Mott are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other.
