# The Cheesemongers' Standoff

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 6 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 17 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `b1ba6858a21b`

## Introduction

A case of opinionated cheeses, their equally opinionated makers, and one display that has begun to smell like a political scandal.
Six cheeses are involved. Determine each cheese's price, maker, rind and ageing.
- **Cheese**: Oakmoss, Winter Tarn, Fenland Blue, Bellringer, Glebe Farmhouse and Old Thrashing.
- **Price** (listed from cheaper to dearer): $3, $5, $7, $9, $11 and $13.
- **Maker**: Perdita, Sigrid, Teodoro, Lachlan, Hepzibah and Ambrose.
- **Rind**: a waxed rind, a cloth-bound rind, an ash-coated rind, a washed rind, a natural rind and a cider-washed rind.
- **Aged for**: nine months, five months, three weeks, two years, thirty months and fourteen months.
Every cheese has a different price, maker, rind and ageing, so within each category every item is used exactly once.
