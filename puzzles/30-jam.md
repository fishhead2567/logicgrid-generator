## Introduction

The preserves tent is the most competitive building at the show, and the only one with a locked door.
Five entrants are involved. Determine each entrant's price, preserve and jar.
- **Entrant**: Drusilla, Emeka, Stanislaw, Ottilie and Harriet.
- **Price** (listed from cheaper to dearer): $3, $5, $7, $9 and $11.
- **Preserve**: damson cheese; rosehip syrup; sloe and apple butter; medlar jam; and green walnut pickle.
- **Jar**: a jar with a cloth lid, an unlabelled jar, a squat kilner, a recycled honey jar and a tall french jar.
Every entrant has a different price, preserve and jar, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The entrant who uses a squat kilner is cheaper than Emeka in price order. Emeka uses a tall french jar. The entrant who uses a tall french jar is cheaper than Harriet in price order.
2. The entrant who uses a recycled honey jar enters green walnut pickle.
3. The entrant who enters rosehip syrup and $11 are exactly one price step apart in price order, in one direction or the other.
4. The entrant who uses an unlabelled jar falls somewhere between the entrant who enters green walnut pickle and $11 in price order.
5. $9 falls somewhere between the entrant who uses a recycled honey jar and the entrant who enters medlar jam in price order.
6. Stanislaw and the entrant who enters sloe and apple butter are exactly one price step apart in price order, in one direction or the other.
7. Drusilla is cheaper than the entrant who enters sloe and apple butter in price order.
8. The entrant who enters green walnut pickle falls somewhere between Emeka and Ottilie in price order. The entrant who uses a recycled honey jar is cheaper than Emeka in price order.
