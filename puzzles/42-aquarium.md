# The Night Shift at Coldwater Hall

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 14 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `d39c10c8426f`

## Introduction

Coldwater Hall Aquarium keeps a night rota of tanks, and one keeper who is definitely feeding the wrong things.
Five keepers are involved. Determine each keeper's slot, tank, feed and incident.
- **Keeper**: Agnetha, Oskar, Fitzwilliam, Marisol and Bartolo.
- **Running order** (listed from earlier to later): slot one, slot two, slot three, slot four and slot five.
- **Tank**: the touch pool, the kelp tank, the jellyfish column, the deep reef and the octopus enclosure.
- **Feed**: brine shrimp, nori sheets, mussel meat, a gelatine cube of unclear provenance and chopped squid.
- **Logged incident**: a failed chiller, an unexplained pH swing, a cracked viewing pane, an algal bloom and a blocked skimmer.
Every keeper has a different slot, tank, feed and incident, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. Slot two and the keeper who tends the touch pool both sit at odd-numbered slots or both at even-numbered slots. The keeper who feeds nori sheets does not work slot two.
2. The keeper who tends the octopus enclosure and slot two are exactly three slots apart in slot order. The keeper who feeds chopped squid does not work slot two.
3. Fitzwilliam is later than slot four in slot order.
4. The keeper who logs an unexplained pH swing is earlier than slot four in slot order.
5. The keeper who logs a cracked viewing pane is earlier than slot two in slot order.
6. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The keeper who feeds brine shrimp and slot one are exactly four slots apart in slot order. (b) The keeper who feeds brine shrimp logs a cracked viewing pane.
7. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Marisol is exactly one slot earlier than slot three, with no slot in between. (b) Marisol logs a blocked skimmer.
8. The keeper who feeds mussel meat falls somewhere between Marisol and slot five in slot order.
9. The keeper who feeds chopped squid falls somewhere between the keeper who logs a cracked viewing pane and the keeper who logs a failed chiller in slot order.
10. The keeper who logs an algal bloom is earlier than Bartolo in slot order.
11. Oskar and the keeper who tends the kelp tank are exactly one slot apart in slot order, in one direction or the other.
12. The following two do NOT both hold; either one alone may hold, and so may neither: (a) The keeper who feeds brine shrimp tends the deep reef. (b) The keeper who feeds chopped squid logs an unexplained pH swing.
13. The keeper who tends the jellyfish column falls somewhere between the keeper who logs a failed chiller and Bartolo in slot order.
14. Oskar does not feed chopped squid.
