# The Case of the Missing Trowel

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 6 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 17 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `7e5abe865022`

## Introduction

The Marchmont Mutual Improvement Society has taken on the case of the missing trowel with a seriousness it has never shown a book.
Six investigators are involved. Determine each investigator's start time, theory, clue and alibi.
- **Investigator**: Ngozi, Fergus, Ariadne, Hortensia, Leopold and Barnabas.
- **Start time** (listed from earlier to later): 8am, 9am, 10am, 11am, noon and 1pm.
- **Theory**: a clerical error, an inside job, a jealous rival, a conspiracy of two, nothing happened at all and a magpie.
- **Clue produced**: a tyre mark, a torn receipt, a smell of creosote, an unexplained hole, a bootprint and a dropped glove.
- **Alibi**: the dentist, watching the snooker, choir practice, a long bath, a locked greenhouse and no alibi whatsoever.
Every investigator has a different start time, theory, clue and alibi, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

6. The investigator who favours a magpie and 8am are exactly four hours apart in start time order.
7. The investigator who produces a torn receipt and Hortensia are exactly two hours apart in start time order.
8. The investigator who claims a long bath is exactly one hour earlier than Hortensia, with no start time in between.
9. The investigator who claims a locked greenhouse is exactly one hour earlier than Ariadne, with no start time in between.
10. Ngozi does not favour an inside job.
11. The investigator who produces a bootprint and Barnabas are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other.
12. The investigator who claims no alibi whatsoever and the investigator who favours a jealous rival are exactly four hours apart in start time order.
13. The investigator who claims the dentist falls somewhere between the investigator who produces a torn receipt and the investigator who favours nothing happened at all in start time order.
14. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Ariadne produces an unexplained hole. (b) The investigator who claims a long bath and the investigator who produces a tyre mark both sit at odd-numbered hours or both at even-numbered hours.
15. The investigator who produces a smell of creosote and the investigator who favours nothing happened at all are exactly three hours apart in start time order.
16. Leopold and the investigator who favours nothing happened at all are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other.
17. Leopold and the investigator who favours a clerical error are exactly two hours apart in start time order.
