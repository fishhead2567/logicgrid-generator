## Introduction

There are 6 members. Each has exactly one start time and one item from every other category below, and within each category every item belongs to a different member.
- **Start time** (listed in order): 8 a.m., 9 a.m., 10 a.m., 11 a.m., noon and 1 p.m..
- **Investigator**: Fergus, Ariadne, Hortensia, Immaculata, Ngozi and Barnabas.
- **Theory**: a clerical error, an inside job, a jealous rival, a conspiracy of two, nothing happened at all and a magpie.
- **Clue produced**: a tyre mark, a torn receipt, a smell of creosote, an unexplained hole, a bootprint and a dropped glove.
- **Alibi**: the dentist, watching the snooker, choir practice, a long bath, a locked greenhouse and no alibi whatsoever.
The hours above are listed in order, from 8 a.m. to 1 p.m..
