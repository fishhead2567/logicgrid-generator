# Christmas Morning on Lark Street

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 13 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `0ecdd7a57437`

## Introduction

A pile of wish lists, and one set of parents trying very hard to remember who asked for what.
Five kids are involved. Determine each kid's age, toy, pajamas and snack.
- **Kid**: Deshawn, Frankie, Rosalind, Yuki and Alton.
- **Age** (listed from younger to older): 8 years old, 9 years old, 10 years old, 11 years old and 12 years old.
- **Toy**: a karaoke machine, an electric keyboard, a pink skateboard, a bug-collecting kit and a telescope.
- **Pajamas**: orange striped pajamas, reindeer pajamas, blue star pajamas, snowflake pajamas and yellow footed pajamas.
- **Snack**: sugar cookies, a candy cane, a slice of fruitcake, oatmeal cookies and carrot sticks.
Every kid has a different age, toy, pajamas and snack, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. 11 years old is exactly one year younger than the kid who leaves out oatmeal cookies, with no age in between. The kid who hopes for a bug-collecting kit is 11 years old.
2. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The kid who wears reindeer pajamas is not 11 years old. (b) The kid who hopes for a telescope is not 12 years old.
3. The kid who leaves out sugar cookies and 8 years old are exactly one year apart in age order, in one direction or the other.
4. 9 years old is exactly one year younger than the kid who wears yellow footed pajamas, with no age in between.
5. 10 years old is exactly one year younger than Alton, with no age in between.
6. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The kid who wears orange striped pajamas is younger than the kid who leaves out oatmeal cookies in age order. (b) The kid who wears blue star pajamas is 10 years old.
7. The following two do NOT both hold; either one alone may hold, and so may neither: (a) The kid who wears reindeer pajamas and the kid who leaves out carrot sticks are exactly one year apart in age order, in one direction or the other. (b) The kid who wears blue star pajamas hopes for a bug-collecting kit.
8. The kid who leaves out a candy cane and the kid who hopes for a pink skateboard are exactly one year apart in age order, in one direction or the other.
9. Rosalind is younger than the kid who hopes for a telescope in age order.
10. The kid who hopes for a karaoke machine is exactly one year younger than Deshawn, with no age in between.
11. The kid who wears snowflake pajamas and the kid who leaves out sugar cookies are exactly one year apart in age order, in one direction or the other.
12. Yuki is younger than the kid who leaves out a slice of fruitcake in age order.
13. Rosalind is younger than the kid who leaves out carrot sticks in age order. Rosalind does not wear snowflake pajamas. Rosalind is younger than the kid who wears blue star pajamas in age order.
