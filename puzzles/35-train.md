# The Loft Layout Dispute

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 12 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `a724e71f6cf3`

## Introduction

The members of the model railway society share one loft layout and a deep well of resentment.
Five modellers are involved. Determine each modeller's shelf, stock, scenery and grievance.
- **Modeller**: Cuthbert, Abiodun, Hendrik, Rosamond and Lavinia.
- **Shelf** (listed from lower to higher): the bottom shelf, the second shelf, the third shelf, the fourth shelf and the fifth shelf.
- **Rolling stock**: a scratch-built crane wagon, a battered autocoach, a gleaming Pacific, a rake of milk tankers and a kit-built brake van.
- **Scenery**: a flocked cutting, a cardboard viaduct, a coal staithe, a plaster tunnel mouth and a river of resin.
- **Grievance**: somebody else's couplings, the choice of era, the timetable, the wiring and the lighting.
Every modeller has a different shelf, stock, scenery and grievance, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The modeller who owns a kit-built brake van and the fourth shelf are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other. (b) The third shelf and the modeller who owns a kit-built brake van both sit at odd-numbered shelves or both at even-numbered shelves.
2. Cuthbert and the fourth shelf are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other.
3. The modeller who complains about the timetable and the fourth shelf are exactly three shelves apart in shelf order.
4. The modeller who builds a flocked cutting and the fourth shelf are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other.
5. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Rosamond is lower than the modeller who complains about the timetable in shelf order. (b) The modeller who complains about the choice of era owns a rake of milk tankers.
6. The modeller who complains about somebody else's couplings and the modeller who builds a river of resin are exactly two shelves apart in shelf order.
7. The modeller who owns a gleaming Pacific falls somewhere between the modeller who builds a coal staithe and the modeller who builds a plaster tunnel mouth in shelf order.
8. The modeller who complains about the lighting does not build a river of resin.
9. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Lavinia is lower than the modeller who owns a rake of milk tankers in shelf order. (b) The modeller who complains about the choice of era is exactly one shelf lower than the modeller who builds a coal staithe, with no shelf in between.
10. The modeller who complains about somebody else's couplings and Abiodun are exactly four shelves apart in shelf order.
11. Lavinia is exactly one shelf lower than the modeller who complains about the lighting, with no shelf in between.
12. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The modeller who complains about the timetable and Abiodun are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other. (b) Rosamond is exactly one shelf lower than the modeller who owns a battered autocoach, with no shelf in between.
