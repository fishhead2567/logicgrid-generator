# The Mislabelled Drawers

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 4 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 9 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `e93f580ea8e8`

## Introduction

The Vance Municipal Archive was reorganised in 1974 by somebody who has since left no forwarding address.
Five archivists are involved. Determine each archivist's shelf, holding and decade.
- **Archivist**: Ms Okafor, Mr Pelletier, Dr Lindqvist, Mr Ravensworth and Dr Mwangi.
- **Shelf** (listed from lower to higher): the bottom shelf, the second shelf, the third shelf, the fourth shelf and the fifth shelf.
- **Holding**: a box of unopened wills, a folder of libel writs, the workhouse admissions book, a set of tram timetables and the smallpox ledgers.
- **Decade**: the 1860s, the 1970s, the 1910s, the 1890s and the 1830s.
Every archivist has a different shelf, holding and decade, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The second shelf falls somewhere between the archivist who catalogues a folder of libel writs and the archivist who catalogues a set of tram timetables in shelf order.
2. The archivist who catalogues the workhouse admissions book and the fourth shelf are exactly two shelves apart in shelf order.
3. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Ms Okafor is higher than the fourth shelf in shelf order. (b) Mr Ravensworth catalogues the smallpox ledgers.
4. The archivist who covers the 1830s falls somewhere between the archivist who catalogues a folder of libel writs and the bottom shelf in shelf order.
5. Mr Ravensworth and the second shelf are exactly three shelves apart in shelf order. Mr Ravensworth does not cover the 1890s.
6. The archivist who covers the 1910s and the fourth shelf are exactly three shelves apart in shelf order.
7. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Dr Mwangi is lower than the archivist who catalogues a folder of libel writs in shelf order. (b) Mr Pelletier does not cover the 1910s.
8. The archivist who covers the 1860s falls somewhere between Mr Pelletier and Mr Ravensworth in shelf order. The archivist who covers the 1860s is lower than Dr Mwangi in shelf order.
9. The archivist who catalogues a set of tram timetables is exactly one shelf lower than Dr Lindqvist, with no shelf in between.
