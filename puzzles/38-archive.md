# The Mislabelled Drawers

*The Vance Municipal Archive was reorganised in 1974 by somebody who has since left no forwarding address.*

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 4 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 7 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `39338b0eee35`

## Introduction

There are 5 drawers. Each has exactly one shelf and one item from every other category below, and within each category every item belongs to a different drawer.
- **Shelf** (listed in order): the bottom shelf, the second shelf, the third shelf, the fourth shelf and the fifth shelf.
- **Holding**: the harbour dredging reports, the workhouse admissions book, the smallpox ledgers, a box of unopened wills and the lost property register.
- **Decade**: the 1860s, the 1950s, the 1940s, the 1910s and the 1890s.
- **Condition**: water-damaged, bound upside down, fire-scorched, mouse-chewed and foxed.
The shelves above are listed in order, from the bottom shelf to the fifth shelf.

## Clues

1. The 1860s goes with the lost property register.
2. The fourth shelf is lower than a box of unopened wills' shelf. The third shelf falls somewhere between water-damaged and a box of unopened wills in shelf order. Foxed falls somewhere between the fourth shelf and the second shelf in shelf order.
3. The bottom shelf is lower than bound upside down's shelf. Water-damaged does not go with the bottom shelf.
4. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Water-damaged's shelf is lower than the 1860s' shelf. (b) Water-damaged does not go with the second shelf.
5. Mouse-chewed falls somewhere between the 1940s and a box of unopened wills in shelf order. The 1950s' shelf is immediately lower than a box of unopened wills' shelf, with no shelf in between.
6. The 1910s' shelf is lower than the smallpox ledgers' shelf.
7. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Fire-scorched and the 1950s are next to each other in shelf order, in one order or the other. (b) The 1860s' shelf is immediately lower than the harbour dredging reports' shelf, with no shelf in between.
