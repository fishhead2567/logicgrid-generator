# The Crossings to Inverlaig

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 12 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `6d150f109290`

## Introduction

The Inverlaig ferry runs a full day of crossings in theory, and a variable number in practice.
Five skippers are involved. Determine each skipper's start time, cargo, vessel and reason.
- **Skipper**: Fearghas, Tormod, Ruaridh, Senga and Calla.
- **Start time** (listed from earlier to later): 8am, 9am, 10am, 11am and noon.
- **Notable cargo**: nineteen sheep, a refrigerated van, a consignment of bees, a wedding party and a brass band.
- **Vessel**: the Kelpie, the Tern, the Three Brothers, the Ailsa Belle and the Steadfast.
- **Delay blamed on**: a fouled propeller, a slipway repair, paperwork, a missing deckhand and fog.
Every skipper has a different start time, cargo, vessel and reason, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The skipper who carries a brass band sails at 10am. (b) One of 11am and the skipper who commands the Steadfast sits at an odd-numbered start time and the other at an even-numbered start time.
2. The skipper who carries a consignment of bees and 9am are exactly three hours apart in start time order.
3. Tormod falls somewhere between 9am and the skipper who commands the Steadfast in start time order.
4. The skipper who blames a slipway repair and 8am are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other.
5. The skipper who carries a refrigerated van and noon are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other.
6. The skipper who commands the Three Brothers falls somewhere between the skipper who blames paperwork and 11am in start time order.
7. Senga does not blame a missing deckhand. Senga and the skipper who commands the Steadfast are exactly one hour apart in start time order, in one direction or the other.
8. The skipper who blames a slipway repair falls somewhere between the skipper who carries a wedding party and the skipper who commands the Tern in start time order.
9. Ruaridh does not carry a consignment of bees.
10. The skipper who carries a brass band is exactly one hour earlier than the skipper who blames a slipway repair, with no start time in between.
11. Fearghas is exactly one hour earlier than the skipper who blames a fouled propeller, with no start time in between.
12. The skipper who carries a brass band and the skipper who commands the Ailsa Belle are exactly two hours apart in start time order.
