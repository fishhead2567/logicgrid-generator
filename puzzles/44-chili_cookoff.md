## Introduction

The volunteer fire department holds a chili cook-off every fall, and the judging has never once ended quietly.
Six cooks are involved. Determine each cook's placing, chili, secret and topping.
- **Cook**: Bobbi Jo, Hollis, Sunny Deel, Luis Ortega, Verna and Gracie Mapp.
- **Placing** (listed from better to worse): first, second, third, fourth, fifth and sixth.
- **Chili**: a smoky chipotle chili, a plain old beef chili, a brisket chili, a green chile stew, a white chicken chili and a five-bean chili.
- **Secret**: a splash of cider vinegar, fire-roasted corn, cinnamon, a square of dark chocolate, nothing but salt and cold coffee.
- **Topping**: oyster crackers, shredded cheddar, crushed corn chips, a fried egg, scallions and sour cream.
Every cook has a different placing, chili, secret and topping, so within each category every item is used exactly once.
