## Introduction

The preserves tent is the most competitive building at the show, and the only one with a locked door.
Six entrants are involved. Determine each entrant's price, preserve, jar and ingredient.
- **Entrant**: Drusilla, Harriet, Bernadette, Ottilie, Rafferty and Emeka.
- **Price** (listed from cheaper to dearer): $3, $5, $7, $9, $11 and $13.
- **Preserve**: sloe and apple butter; medlar jam; hot pepper jelly; damson cheese; quince jelly; and rosehip syrup.
- **Jar**: a squat kilner, an unlabelled jar, a recycled honey jar, a jar with a cloth lid, a hexagonal jar and a tall french jar.
- **Secret ingredient**: orange zest, a splash of gin, nothing whatsoever, a spoon of treacle, star anise and a bay leaf.
Every entrant has a different price, preserve, jar and ingredient, so within each category every item is used exactly once.
