## Introduction

Coldwater Hall Aquarium keeps a night rota of tanks, and one keeper who is definitely feeding the wrong things.
Five keepers are involved. Determine each keeper's slot, tank, feed and incident.
- **Keeper**: Rhiannon, Oskar, Fitzwilliam, Chiamaka and Marisol.
- **Running order** (listed from earlier to later): slot one, slot two, slot three, slot four and slot five.
- **Tank**: the deep reef, the seahorse nursery, the estuary tank, the kelp tank and the touch pool.
- **Feed**: a gelatine cube of unclear provenance, frozen krill, brine shrimp, nori sheets and mussel meat.
- **Logged incident**: an algal bloom, a failed chiller, an unexplained pH swing, a cracked viewing pane and a visitor's dropped phone.
Every keeper has a different slot, tank, feed and incident, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The keeper who logs a visitor's dropped phone is later than slot four in slot order. (b) The keeper who feeds mussel meat and slot two are exactly two slots apart in slot order.
2. Slot two falls somewhere between the keeper who logs a visitor's dropped phone and Rhiannon in slot order.
3. One of the keeper who logs a visitor's dropped phone and slot five sits at an odd-numbered slot and the other at an even-numbered slot.
4. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The keeper who tends the touch pool is later than slot five in slot order. (b) The keeper who logs a failed chiller is exactly one slot earlier than the keeper who feeds mussel meat, with no slot in between.
5. The keeper who logs a cracked viewing pane and slot two are exactly one slot apart in slot order, in one direction or the other.
6. The keeper who tends the deep reef is exactly one slot earlier than slot four, with no slot in between.
7. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The keeper who tends the kelp tank is earlier than the keeper who feeds brine shrimp in slot order. (b) Chiamaka tends the deep reef.
8. The keeper who tends the estuary tank falls somewhere between the keeper who feeds frozen krill and Oskar in slot order.
9. The keeper who feeds nori sheets and the keeper who logs an unexplained pH swing are exactly one slot apart in slot order, in one direction or the other.
10. The keeper who tends the touch pool is earlier than the keeper who logs a visitor's dropped phone in slot order.
11. Chiamaka does not log an unexplained pH swing.
12. Oskar is earlier than the keeper who tends the seahorse nursery in slot order.
13. The keeper who feeds frozen krill is earlier than the keeper who tends the seahorse nursery in slot order.
14. Fitzwilliam and the keeper who tends the estuary tank are exactly one slot apart in slot order, in one direction or the other.
