# The Orchid House Reckoning

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 16 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `1e2919e232fa`

## Introduction

The orchid house at Fenn Park is shared by its growers, each convinced the others are overwatering.
Five growers are involved. Determine each grower's harvest month, orchid, potting mix and pest.
- **Grower**: Taavi, Columbine, Ambrose, Sigismund and Rowena.
- **Harvest month** (listed from earlier to later): January, February, March, April and May.
- **Orchid**: a jewel orchid, a bucket orchid, a ghost orchid, a dancing lady and a moth orchid.
- **Potting mix**: tree fern fibre, bare root on cork, coconut husk, sphagnum moss and bark chip.
- **Pest**: root rot, mealybug, fungus gnats, red spider mite and a persistent slug.
Every grower has a different harvest month, orchid, potting mix and pest, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Sigismund harvests in March. (b) The grower who pots in coconut husk and April both sit at odd-numbered months or both at even-numbered months.
2. The following two do NOT both hold; either one alone may hold, and so may neither: (a) Ambrose and May are exactly one month apart in harvest month order, in one direction or the other. (b) Ambrose grows a ghost orchid.
3. The grower who grows a dancing lady does not harvest in February. Ambrose does not grow a dancing lady.
4. The grower who is troubled by red spider mite and March are exactly two months apart in harvest month order.
5. Columbine does not grow a ghost orchid. The grower who grows a ghost orchid is earlier than Sigismund in harvest month order.
6. The following is NOT true: Ambrose is earlier than the grower who grows a ghost orchid in harvest month order.
7. The grower who grows a dancing lady is earlier than the grower who is troubled by a persistent slug in harvest month order.
8. Rowena and the grower who is troubled by a persistent slug are exactly three months apart in harvest month order.
9. The grower who pots in sphagnum moss and the grower who grows a dancing lady are exactly one month apart in harvest month order, in one direction or the other.
10. Sigismund and the grower who is troubled by root rot are exactly three months apart in harvest month order.
11. Taavi falls somewhere between the grower who grows a bucket orchid and the grower who grows a ghost orchid in harvest month order.
12. The grower who pots in bare root on cork and the grower who is troubled by mealybug are exactly two months apart in harvest month order.
13. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) Ambrose is exactly one month earlier than the grower who pots in bark chip, with no harvest month in between. (b) Ambrose and the grower who pots in bare root on cork both sit at odd-numbered months or both at even-numbered months.
14. The grower who pots in bark chip and the grower who grows a moth orchid are exactly one month apart in harvest month order, in one direction or the other.
15. The grower who pots in bare root on cork and the grower who grows a ghost orchid are exactly three months apart in harvest month order.
16. Rowena and the grower who pots in bark chip are exactly two months apart in harvest month order.
