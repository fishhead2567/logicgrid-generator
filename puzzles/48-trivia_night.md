# Trivia Night at the Public Library

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 6 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 16 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `1990c2c4701f`

## Introduction

The library runs trivia on the first Thursday of the month, and the scorekeeper will not be hurried.
Six players are involved. Determine each player's score, team, category and charm.
- **Player**: Chet, Takeshi, Priscilla, Vikram, Darby and Lou Ann.
- **Score** (listed from lower to higher): 61 points, 64 points, 67 points, 70 points, 73 points and 76 points.
- **Team**: the Quizzard of Oz, Nacho Average Team, Les Quizerables, the Wi-Fighters, the Smarty Pants and the Library Cards.
- **Category**: breakfast cereals, dinosaurs, state capitals, one-hit wonders, world flags and cloud formations.
- **Charm**: a laminated periodic table, a lucky pencil, a thermos of cocoa, a four-leaf clover in a locket, a stuffed owl and absolutely nothing.
Every player has a different score, team, category and charm, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) 70 points is exactly one score step lower than the player who takes the questions on breakfast cereals, with no score in between. (b) The player who takes the questions on state capitals scores 61 points.
2. 64 points is exactly one score step lower than Lou Ann, with no score in between. 64 points falls somewhere between Vikram and the player who plays for the Wi-Fighters in score order.
3. 67 points falls somewhere between the player who brings along a thermos of cocoa and the player who plays for the Smarty Pants in score order.
4. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) Darby brings along a four-leaf clover in a locket. (b) Darby scores 73 points.
5. 73 points has a score exactly 12 points more than the player who plays for Nacho Average Team.
6. 70 points has a score exactly 9 points more than the player who brings along a stuffed owl.
7. Priscilla and the player who plays for Les Quizerables are exactly one score step apart in score order, in one direction or the other.
8. The player who takes the questions on dinosaurs is lower than the player who brings along a four-leaf clover in a locket in score order.
9. The player who plays for the Library Cards is exactly one score step lower than the player who brings along a lucky pencil, with no score in between.
10. The player who takes the questions on state capitals is exactly one score step lower than the player who plays for the Wi-Fighters, with no score in between.
11. The player who plays for the Library Cards has a score exactly 6 points more than the player who takes the questions on world flags.
12. The following two do NOT both hold; either one alone may hold, and so may neither: (a) Takeshi brings along absolutely nothing. (b) Priscilla does not bring along a four-leaf clover in a locket.
13. Priscilla has a score exactly 12 points more than the player who takes the questions on one-hit wonders.
14. The player who plays for the Library Cards falls somewhere between the player who brings along a four-leaf clover in a locket and the player who brings along absolutely nothing in score order.
15. The player who takes the questions on breakfast cereals does not bring along a thermos of cocoa.
16. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Takeshi is lower than the player who takes the questions on dinosaurs in score order. (b) Takeshi is lower than the player who takes the questions on world flags in score order.
