# A Benchful of Clocks, and No Agreement

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 14 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `4ce9d9b1ba63`

## Introduction

Mr Halloran repairs clocks. Those on the bench are all insisting on a different hour.
Five clocks are involved. Determine each clock's shelf, owner, fault and chime.
- **Clock**: a longcase clock, a cuckoo clock, a carriage clock, a station clock and an anniversary clock.
- **Shelf** (listed from lower to higher): the bottom shelf, the second shelf, the third shelf, the fourth shelf and the fifth shelf.
- **Owner**: Dr Vasquez, Mr Dziedzic, Mrs Oyelaran, Father Brennan and Ms Abernathy.
- **Fault**: a seized fusee, a missing click spring, a cracked suspension spring, a snapped mainspring and a worn escape wheel.
- **Chime**: a bell and gong; a ship's bell; no chime at all; a single strike; and Whittington.
Every clock has a different shelf, owner, fault and chime, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. An anniversary clock sounds a single strike.
2. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) A carriage clock sits on the fifth shelf. (b) A station clock and the fourth shelf both sit at odd-numbered shelves or both at even-numbered shelves.
3. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The clock that suffers from a cracked suspension spring is higher than the fourth shelf in shelf order. (b) The clock that sounds no chime at all and the second shelf both sit at odd-numbered shelves or both at even-numbered shelves.
4. The clock that suffers from a missing click spring and the third shelf are exactly two shelves apart in shelf order.
5. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The clock that suffers from a seized fusee is higher than the second shelf in shelf order. (b) The clock that suffers from a snapped mainspring belongs to Mrs Oyelaran.
6. The fourth shelf falls somewhere between the clock that sounds a bell and gong and the clock that sounds a single strike in shelf order.
7. The fourth shelf falls somewhere between a cuckoo clock and a station clock in shelf order.
8. The clock that belongs to Ms Abernathy is lower than the second shelf in shelf order.
9. The clock that sounds Whittington and a longcase clock are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other.
10. The clock that sounds a bell and gong and the clock that belongs to Dr Vasquez are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other.
11. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The clock that belongs to Mrs Oyelaran is lower than the clock that sounds a single strike in shelf order. (b) The clock that sounds a ship's bell suffers from a snapped mainspring.
12. The clock that belongs to Father Brennan is lower than a carriage clock in shelf order.
13. A carriage clock is exactly one shelf lower than the clock that suffers from a snapped mainspring, with no shelf in between.
14. The clock that suffers from a worn escape wheel is exactly one shelf lower than a cuckoo clock, with no shelf in between.
