# The Loft Layout Dispute

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 14 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `877b1dc35ecd`

## Introduction

The members of the model railway society share one loft layout and a deep well of resentment.
Five modellers are involved. Determine each modeller's shelf, stock, scenery and grievance.
- **Modeller**: Abiodun, Hendrik, Rosamond, Gilbert and Esperanza.
- **Shelf** (listed from lower to higher): the bottom shelf, the second shelf, the third shelf, the fourth shelf and the fifth shelf.
- **Rolling stock**: an unpainted shunter, a rake of milk tankers, a kit-built brake van, a brass pannier tank and a battered autocoach.
- **Scenery**: a flocked cutting, a plaster tunnel mouth, a cardboard viaduct, a sheet of static grass and a working level crossing.
- **Grievance**: somebody else's couplings, a missing screwdriver, the lighting, the wiring and the timetable.
Every modeller has a different shelf, stock, scenery and grievance, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The modeller who builds a working level crossing does not store on the fifth shelf. Hendrik falls somewhere between the bottom shelf and the fifth shelf in shelf order.
2. The modeller who builds a flocked cutting and the bottom shelf are exactly two shelves apart in shelf order.
3. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Gilbert and the modeller who owns a brass pannier tank are exactly four shelves apart in shelf order. (b) Abiodun stores on the second shelf.
4. The modeller who complains about the timetable and the bottom shelf are exactly four shelves apart in shelf order.
5. The modeller who owns a rake of milk tankers is exactly one shelf lower than the second shelf, with no shelf in between.
6. The modeller who builds a plaster tunnel mouth and the modeller who owns a brass pannier tank are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other.
7. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Hendrik and the modeller who owns a kit-built brake van are exactly one shelf apart in shelf order, in one direction or the other. (b) The modeller who builds a sheet of static grass and the modeller who owns an unpainted shunter both sit at odd-numbered shelves or both at even-numbered shelves.
8. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Rosamond is lower than the modeller who owns an unpainted shunter in shelf order. (b) The modeller who complains about the lighting and the modeller who owns an unpainted shunter are exactly two shelves apart in shelf order.
9. One of the modeller who complains about a missing screwdriver and the modeller who owns a kit-built brake van sits at an odd-numbered shelf and the other at an even-numbered shelf.
10. The modeller who complains about the wiring does not own a rake of milk tankers.
11. Hendrik is lower than the modeller who builds a flocked cutting in shelf order.
12. Rosamond falls somewhere between the modeller who builds a sheet of static grass and the modeller who owns a kit-built brake van in shelf order.
13. Esperanza does not build a cardboard viaduct.
14. The modeller who builds a sheet of static grass is exactly one shelf lower than the modeller who complains about a missing screwdriver, with no shelf in between.
