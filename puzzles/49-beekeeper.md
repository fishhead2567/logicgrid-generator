# The Honey Show Scandal

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 6 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 18 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `cff23af7210c`

## Introduction

The annual honey show has been judged, and at least two people are writing letters about it.
Six beekeepers are involved. Determine each beekeeper's entry fee, honey, hive and note.
- **Beekeeper**: Wilhelmina, Priya, Ezekiel, Mungo, Breda and Antonina.
- **Entry fee** (listed from cheaper to dearer): $2, $4, $6, $8, $10 and $12.
- **Honey**: clover honey, borage honey, lime blossom honey, heather honey, chestnut honey and sea lavender honey.
- **Hive type**: a national hive, a WBC hive, a Langstroth hive, a Warre hive, a long deep hive and a top-bar hive.
- **Judge's note**: an overbearing aroma, a cloudy jar, crystallised early, an unlabelled lid, slightly over-filtered and a trace of wax.
Every beekeeper has a different entry fee, honey, hive and note, so within each category every item is used exactly once.
