# The Mudflat Regatta

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 16 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `92efe43ad662`

## Introduction

The Mudflat Regatta is rowed over a course that is only a course at high tide, which is where most of the trouble starts.
Five crews are involved. Determine each crew's placing, stroke, boat and colours.
- **Crew**: the Gasworks crew, the Seminary crew, the Brickworks crew, the Customs crew and the Tannery crew.
- **Placing** (listed from better to worse): first, second, third, fourth and fifth.
- **Stroke**: Yusuf, Thomasina, Crispin, Ondine and Maeve.
- **Boat**: the Peregrine, the Mudlark, the Last Resort, the Gilded Eel and the Obstinate.
- **Colours**: mustard and black; bottle green; lilac and bronze; navy with a white band; and scarlet.
Every crew has a different placing, stroke, boat and colours, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The crew who rows the Last Resort and second are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other. (b) The crew who is stroked by Thomasina finishes second.
2. The crew who is stroked by Crispin and third are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other.
3. The crew who rows the Obstinate and fifth are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other.
4. Fourth falls somewhere between the crew who rows the Mudlark and the crew who rows the Peregrine in placing order.
5. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The Seminary crew rows the Obstinate. (b) The crew who wears navy with a white band and first both sit at odd-numbered places or both at even-numbered places.
6. Fourth falls somewhere between the crew who rows the Peregrine and the crew who is stroked by Maeve in placing order. The Tannery crew does not row the Peregrine.
7. The crew who is stroked by Ondine is exactly one place better than second, with no placing in between.
8. Fourth falls somewhere between the crew who wears bottle green and the Gasworks crew in placing order.
9. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) The crew who wears mustard and black is worse than first in placing order. (b) The Customs crew and first are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other.
10. The crew who is stroked by Thomasina is exactly one place better than the crew who rows the Peregrine, with no placing in between.
11. The following two do NOT both hold; either one alone may hold, and so may neither: (a) The crew who wears mustard and black and the crew who is stroked by Yusuf are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other. (b) The Brickworks crew and the crew who is stroked by Yusuf are exactly four places apart in placing order.
12. The crew who wears scarlet is better than the crew who rows the Obstinate in placing order.
13. The crew who wears bottle green and the Brickworks crew are exactly four places apart in placing order.
14. The crew who wears navy with a white band falls somewhere between the crew who rows the Last Resort and the crew who rows the Peregrine in placing order.
15. The crew who rows the Mudlark is better than the Gasworks crew in placing order.
16. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) The crew who is stroked by Ondine is better than the crew who wears lilac and bronze in placing order. (b) The crew who rows the Gilded Eel is exactly one place better than the Customs crew, with no placing in between.
