# The Mudflat Regatta

**Difficulty** 7/7 (expert) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 14 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `92efe43ad662`

## Introduction

The Mudflat Regatta is rowed over a course that is only a course at high tide, which is where most of the trouble starts.
Five crews are involved. Determine each crew's placing, stroke, boat and colours.
- **Crew**: the Gasworks crew, the Seminary crew, the Brickworks crew, the Customs crew and the Tannery crew.
- **Placing** (listed from better to worse): first, second, third, fourth and fifth.
- **Stroke**: Yusuf, Thomasina, Crispin, Ondine and Maeve.
- **Boat**: the Peregrine, the Mudlark, the Last Resort, the Gilded Eel and the Obstinate.
- **Colours**: mustard and black; bottle green; lilac and bronze; navy with a white band; and scarlet.
Every crew has a different placing, stroke, boat and colours, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The crew who is stroked by Crispin and third are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other.
2. The crew who is stroked by Thomasina falls somewhere between the crew who rows the Mudlark and fifth in placing order.
3. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The crew who rows the Peregrine and the crew who is stroked by Maeve are exactly two places apart in placing order. (b) The crew who is stroked by Ondine finishes second.
4. The Tannery crew is better than the crew who is stroked by Yusuf in placing order. The Tannery crew does not finish fourth.
5. The crew who rows the Gilded Eel is exactly one place better than the crew who wears lilac and bronze, with no placing in between. The crew who wears lilac and bronze does not finish second.
6. The crew who rows the Gilded Eel and the crew who is stroked by Crispin are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other.
7. One of the crew who wears mustard and black and the Tannery crew sits at an odd-numbered placing and the other at an even-numbered placing.
8. The crew who wears mustard and black and the Seminary crew are exactly four places apart in placing order.
9. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The crew who rows the Obstinate and the Seminary crew are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other. (b) The crew who rows the Mudlark wears bottle green.
10. The crew who rows the Obstinate and the crew who is stroked by Maeve are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other.
11. The crew who rows the Peregrine and the Brickworks crew are exactly four places apart in placing order.
12. If (a) holds then (b) also holds, but not necessarily the reverse: (a) The crew who rows the Mudlark wears bottle green. (b) The crew who wears mustard and black and the crew who is stroked by Yusuf are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other.
13. One of the crew who rows the Gilded Eel and the Customs crew sits at an odd-numbered placing and the other at an even-numbered placing.
14. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The Gasworks crew and the crew who is stroked by Yusuf are exactly one place apart in placing order, in one direction or the other. (b) The crew who rows the Last Resort and the crew who wears navy with a white band are exactly two places apart in placing order.
