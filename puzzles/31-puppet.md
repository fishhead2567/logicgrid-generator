# A Fortnight at the Little Vesper Theatre

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 14 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `6203e2561060`

## Introduction

The Little Vesper Theatre seats forty people and employs a company of puppeteers who will not be in the same room together.
Five puppeteers are involved. Determine each puppeteer's slot, puppet, play and accompaniment.
- **Puppeteer**: Bowen, Lorcan, Mirabel, Josefa and Stellan.
- **Running order** (listed from earlier to later): slot one, slot two, slot three, slot four and slot five.
- **Lead puppet**: a tin soldier, a one-eyed cat, a shepherd boy, a drowned sailor and a paper crane.
- **Play**: The Clockwork Orchard, Marrowbone, Hollowmas, Six Spoons and The Glass Tide.
- **Accompaniment**: a glass harmonica, a prepared piano, two kettle drums, a bowed psaltery and a hurdy-gurdy.
Every puppeteer has a different slot, puppet, play and accompaniment, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Stellan and slot five are exactly three slots apart in slot order. (b) Lorcan performs in slot three.
2. Bowen is later than slot four in slot order. The puppeteer who works a one-eyed cat is earlier than slot four in slot order.
3. Josefa and slot two are exactly one slot apart in slot order, in one direction or the other.
4. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The puppeteer who stages Hollowmas is earlier than the puppeteer who works a one-eyed cat in slot order. (b) The puppeteer who stages The Glass Tide is later than slot three in slot order.
5. The puppeteer who stages Six Spoons is exactly one slot earlier than slot three, with no slot in between.
6. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The puppeteer who is accompanied by a prepared piano and the puppeteer who stages Marrowbone are exactly one slot apart in slot order, in one direction or the other. (b) The puppeteer who is accompanied by a bowed psaltery is later than slot one in slot order.
7. Stellan is exactly one slot earlier than the puppeteer who stages The Glass Tide, with no slot in between.
8. One of the puppeteer who stages Six Spoons and Mirabel sits at an odd-numbered slot and the other at an even-numbered slot.
9. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Josefa is earlier than the puppeteer who stages Hollowmas in slot order. (b) The puppeteer who works a paper crane and Josefa are exactly two slots apart in slot order.
10. One of the puppeteer who is accompanied by a bowed psaltery and the puppeteer who works a shepherd boy sits at an odd-numbered slot and the other at an even-numbered slot.
11. The puppeteer who stages Marrowbone is exactly one slot earlier than the puppeteer who is accompanied by two kettle drums, with no slot in between.
12. The puppeteer who is accompanied by a hurdy-gurdy and Josefa are exactly one slot apart in slot order, in one direction or the other.
13. The puppeteer who is accompanied by a prepared piano and Mirabel are exactly one slot apart in slot order, in one direction or the other.
14. Lorcan falls somewhere between the puppeteer who works a shepherd boy and the puppeteer who works a tin soldier in slot order.
