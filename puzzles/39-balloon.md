# The Thistledown Balloon Ascent

*The Thistledown Ascent is less a race than a slow argument conducted at two thousand feet.*

**Difficulty** 6/7 (hard) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Grid** 5 x 5 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Clues** 10 &nbsp;&nbsp; **id** `b23439064a73`

## Introduction

There are 5 balloons. Each has exactly one start time and one item from every other category below, and within each category every item belongs to a different balloon.
- **Start time** (listed in order): 8 a.m., 9 a.m., 10 a.m., 11 a.m. and noon.
- **Pilot**: Horatio, Anneliese, Isolde, Grover and Montague.
- **Envelope**: the saffron envelope, the oxblood envelope, the striped envelope, the patchwork envelope and the kingfisher envelope.
- **Landed at**: a cabbage field, a flooded quarry, a caravan park, a reservoir bank and a golf course bunker.
- **Ballast**: bottled water, pea gravel, rock salt, dried beans and lead shot.
The hours above are listed in order, from 8 a.m. to noon.

## Clues

1. 9 a.m. falls somewhere between dried beans and rock salt in start time order. Pea gravel falls somewhere between Isolde and 9 a.m. in start time order.
2. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) The saffron envelope and 8 a.m. are exactly four hours apart in start time order. (b) The striped envelope goes with 9 a.m..
3. The striped envelope and 11 a.m. are next to each other in start time order, in one order or the other. A reservoir bank, Anneliese and 11 a.m. are three different balloons. The striped envelope does not go with a golf course bunker.
4. Lead shot falls somewhere between the kingfisher envelope and noon in start time order. The kingfisher envelope and 10 a.m. are next to each other in start time order, in one order or the other. Lead shot and the striped envelope are next to each other in start time order, in one order or the other.
5. Isolde and 10 a.m. both sit at odd-numbered hours or both at even-numbered hours.
6. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) 9 a.m. is earlier than dried beans' start time. (b) Lead shot goes with the kingfisher envelope.
7. At least one of the following holds, and possibly more than one: (a) Dried beans and Isolde are next to each other in start time order, in one order or the other. (b) The kingfisher envelope goes with Montague.
8. A cabbage field's start time is earlier than Horatio's start time. Pea gravel and a cabbage field are next to each other in start time order, in one order or the other.
9. The oxblood envelope's start time is immediately earlier than a reservoir bank's start time, with no start time in between.
10. A flooded quarry and Anneliese are next to each other in start time order, in one order or the other.
