# The Hollow Creek Pie Judging

The Hollow Creek county fair runs its pie judging with more ceremony than the state legislature, and this year five bakers waited under the bunting while the judges worked their way down the table. The bakers are Odette, Bramwell, Marigold, Winnie and Thaddeus. The placings handed out were first, second, third, fourth and fifth, listed here from best to worst. The fillings were pecan, green tomato, rhubarb, mincemeat and sour cherry; the home counties were Quarry Hill, Little Pell, Fenwick, Dunmore and Hobbs Bend; and the crusts were a cut-out star top, a fork-crimped edge, a fluted rim, a crumb topping and a lattice top. Within each of those categories, every member belongs to a different baker, so each placing, each filling, each home county and each crust is used exactly once.

## Clues

1. The baker who uses a fork-crimped edge placed worse than third.
2. Thaddeus placed exactly one place better than the baker who uses a cut-out star top, with no placing in between, and Thaddeus and the baker who bakes mincemeat placed exactly four places apart.
3. Thaddeus placed better than the baker who uses a lattice top.
4. The baker who uses a cut-out star top does not bake green tomato.
5. Odette and the baker who bakes pecan placed exactly four places apart.
6. Bramwell and the baker who uses a crumb topping placed exactly three places apart, and Bramwell placed exactly one place better than the baker from Dunmore, with no placing in between.
7. The baker from Fenwick placed exactly one place better than the baker who bakes sour cherry, with no placing in between, and the baker who bakes sour cherry placed exactly one place better than the baker from Dunmore, again with no placing in between.
8. The baker from Quarry Hill placed somewhere between Winnie and the baker who uses a lattice top, and Winnie and the baker who bakes rhubarb placed exactly one place apart.
9. At least one of the following is true, and possibly both: Marigold is the baker from Quarry Hill, or the baker from Hobbs Bend and the baker who bakes sour cherry both placed at odd-numbered places or both at even-numbered places.
