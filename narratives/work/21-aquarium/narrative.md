# The Night Shift at Coldwater Hall

At Coldwater Hall Aquarium the night rota is a quiet procession of buckets, torches and softly glowing glass, and on this particular evening five keepers each take a single turn around the building. The rota's Running order runs from earliest to latest as slot one, slot two, slot three, slot four and slot five. The five keepers are Oskar, Marisol, Chiamaka, Rhiannon and Fitzwilliam. The Tank each one is responsible for is one of the jellyfish column, the touch pool, the deep reef, the seahorse nursery and the kelp tank, and the Feed each one carries down the corridor is one of live mysis, a gelatine cube of unclear provenance, mussel meat, nori sheets and chopped squid. Every keeper has exactly one slot in the running order, exactly one tank and exactly one feed, and within each of those categories no two keepers share the same member, so each slot, each keeper, each tank and each feed is used exactly once. The night manager insists the rota always works out; your job is to work out how.

## Clues

1. The keeper who tends the touch pool works slot five.
2. The keeper who feeds nori sheets does not work slot three.
3. The keeper who feeds chopped squid works exactly one slot apart from slot one, in one direction or the other.
4. The keeper who feeds mussel meat does not work slot five.
5. Fitzwilliam works exactly one slot later than slot one, with no slot in between.
6. The keeper who feeds live mysis works earlier in the running order than the keeper who tends the deep reef, and the keeper who tends the deep reef works earlier in the running order than Marisol.
7. The keeper who feeds chopped squid works earlier in the running order than the keeper who tends the kelp tank.
8. The keeper who feeds live mysis works earlier in the running order than Chiamaka.
9. The keeper who feeds mussel meat and Rhiannon work exactly one slot apart, in one direction or the other, and Chiamaka works earlier in the running order than the keeper who feeds mussel meat.
10. The keeper who feeds mussel meat and the keeper who tends the jellyfish column work exactly one slot apart, in one direction or the other.
