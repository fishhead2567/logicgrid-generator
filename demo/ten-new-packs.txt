The Quarter-Peal at St Botolph's
================================

Six ringers, six rounds of the Tuesday practice, and a tower captain keeping a ledger of everyone's mistakes.
Five ringers are involved. Determine each ringer's round and slip.
  - Ringer: Lavinia, Ambrose, Prudence, Dorcas and Hesketh.
  - Round (listed from earlier to later): round one, round two, round three, round four and round five.
  - Slip: a clipped backstroke, nothing at all, a dropped rope, an early snap lead and going methodless.
Every ringer has a different round and slip, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

CLUES
-----
 1. Hesketh falls somewhere between round five and round one in round order. The ringer who is blamed for going methodless is later than round one in round order.
 2. Ambrose is later than round three in round order. Round three is exactly one round earlier than the ringer who is blamed for nothing at all, with no round in between.
 3. Dorcas and round four are exactly one round apart in round order, in one direction or the other.
 4. Round two is exactly one round earlier than the ringer who is blamed for a clipped backstroke, with no round in between.
 5. Prudence and the ringer who is blamed for going methodless are exactly one round apart in round order, in one direction or the other.
 6. The ringer who is blamed for a dropped rope is exactly one round earlier than Dorcas, with no round in between.

ANSWER:
  Round: round one, Ringer: Lavinia, Slip: an early snap lead
  Round: round two, Ringer: Hesketh, Slip: a dropped rope
  Round: round three, Ringer: Dorcas, Slip: a clipped backstroke
  Round: round four, Ringer: Prudence, Slip: nothing at all
  Round: round five, Ringer: Ambrose, Slip: going methodless
