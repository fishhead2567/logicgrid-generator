NINE RESKINNED PACKS
==========================================================================

Same logic, same structure, different register. These nine were English
only in their names, a place and a handful of words.

Nine others stay British, because there the Britishness IS the content:
shillings and guineas in the bookshop, damson and medlar in the jam,
pannier tanks and brake vans in the model railway, change ringing,
drystone walling, allotments, sheepdog trials, the heavy events, pomace.

A different seed and profile per pack, so the clue skeletons differ.
==========================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
[hamradio]  Field Day on Cedar Butte
easy, rating 3/7, 4x4, 6 clues
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A club field day spread over a week, one operator a night, and a generator that has its own opinions.
Four operators are involved. Determine each operator's night, band and mode.
- Operator: Marcus, Lorna, Hank and Ingrid.
- Night (listed from earlier to later): Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.
- Band: 10 meters, 6 meters, 20 meters and 15 meters.
- Mode: slow-scan television, PSK31, CW and RTTY.
Every operator has a different night, band and mode, so within each category every item is used exactly once.

 1. Marcus operates PSK31. The operator who works 6 meters is earlier than Marcus in night order.
 2. The operator who operates CW does not operate on Wednesday. The operator who works 15 meters is earlier than Wednesday in night order.
 3. Hank does not operate on Tuesday.
 4. The operator who works 10 meters falls somewhere between the operator who operates RTTY and the operator who operates slow-scan television in night order.
 5. Lorna is exactly one day earlier than the operator who works 20 meters, with no night in between.
 6. Ingrid is earlier than the operator who operates RTTY in night order.

   answer:
     Night            Monday | Tuesday | Wednesday | Thursday
     Operator         Hank | Ingrid | Lorna | Marcus
     Band             15 meters | 10 meters | 6 meters | 20 meters
     Mode             slow-scan television | CW | RTTY | PSK31
